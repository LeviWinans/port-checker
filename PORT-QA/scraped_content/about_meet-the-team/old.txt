Dr. Jones Raised in San Antonio, Dr. Jones brings a unique blend of technical expertise and compassionate care to every patient experience. Dr. Jones earned her medical degree from the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio and went on to complete her anesthesiology residency at Virginia Commonwealth University. During her residency, her husband served as a pilot in the United States Air Force, a chapter of life that reinforced her appreciation for teamwork, adaptability, and service.
