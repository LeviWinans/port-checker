What is a BBL? A Brazilian Butt Lift, or BBL, is a body contouring procedure that uses your own fat to enhance the shape, volume, and balance of the buttocks. Fat is first removed from areas such as the abdomen, flanks, back, or thighs through liposuction, then purified and strategically transferred to create a fuller, more sculpted silhouette. At Heights Plastic Surgery in Houston BBL should never be approached as volume for volume’s sake. The best result depends on the proportion. Waist definition, hip shape, lower-back contour, and the relationship between the buttocks and the rest of the body all matter. The goal is a shape that looks more balanced, more lifted, and more intentional.

Procedure A BBL is performed in two stages. First, liposuction is used to remove fat from selected donor areas. Common areas include the abdomen, flanks, lower back, or thighs, depending on where excess fat is available and where contour improvement will benefit the final result. Once the fat is collected, it is processed and purified. The healthy fat cells are then carefully injected into the buttocks in selected layers and locations to create improved shape, fullness, and balance. The procedure is highly technique-dependent. Volume alone is not what creates a strong result. Placement, symmetry, and restraint matter just as much. A well-executed BBL should improve the overall silhouette, not overpower it.

Results Some improvement is visible immediately, but early swelling can affect both the donor areas and the buttocks, so the final shape takes time to appreciate. Not all transferred fat survives, which is a normal part of the healing process. Final results become more accurate as swelling settles and the retained fat establishes itself over time. Patients may notice: Fuller buttock contours Improved projection A more sculpted waistline Better hip-to-waist balance Smoother transitions through the lower body More proportional overall shape

Recovery Schedule Today BBL recovery requires attention because healing affects both the areas treated with liposuction and the areas where fat was transferred. The first phase of recovery is usually the most demanding. Patients need to be careful about sitting, sleeping positions, and compression instructions. As swelling improves, the new shape becomes easier to appreciate, but full settling takes longer. Recovery is not especially difficult when patients are prepared for it. It is simply more specific than many other cosmetic procedures. Patients can typically expect: Swelling Bruising Soreness Temporary tightness Restricted pressure on the buttocks during early healing Aftercare Aftercare matters with a BBL because transferred fat needs time and support to heal properly. The body is adjusting in more than one area at once. The details matter here. Thoughtful aftercare helps protect the shape created in surgery and supports a smoother recovery overall. Patients are typically instructed to: Wear compression garments as directed Avoid direct pressure on the buttocks during the early recovery period Follow sitting and sleeping guidance carefully Stay well hydrated Avoid strenuous activity until cleared Attend follow-up appointments Follow all post-operative instructions closely

Brazilian Butt Lift Frequently Asked Questions What is a Brazilian butt lift? The Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL) is a cosmetic procedure that uses advanced liposuction and fat grafting techniques to enhance the size and shape of the buttocks and achieve a younger and perkier contour. Men and women who come in for a Brazilian butt lift consultation typically desire slimming down their abdomen and flanks while simultaneously increasing the size of their buttocks. This procedure may be done alone or may be combined with the use of butt implants for an additional boost of volume and contours. Dr. Cara Downey and Dr. Jamal Bullocks are the leading board-certified plastic surgeon in Houston who continuously performs hundreds of successful butt lift procedures on men and women all across Texas. How much does a Brazilian butt lift cost in Houston? The average cost of a Brazilian Butt Lift in Houston typically ranges between $8,000 and $12,000. The total cost for your Brazilian Butt Lift will depend on factors such as your surgeon’s fee, the location of your procedure, and other miscellaneous medical expenses. At Heights Plastic Surgery, we accept cash and all major credit cards. We are also happy to offer financing through CareCredit® for patients. Because a Brazilian butt lift is typically more about cosmetic appearance and is less of a medical necessity, it is not covered by most insurance plans. Am I a good candidate for a BBL surgery? At Heights Plastic Surgery, ideal candidates for a Brazilian Butt Lift experience the following concerns: Naturally small buttocks Asymmetry in the buttocks Disproportionate curves Ideal candidates for a Brazilian Butt Lift are in good general health and have realistic expectations of their potential results. The best way to determine if you are a good candidate for a Brazilian Butt Lift is to schedule your private consultation with Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks. How do you perform BBL surgeries at Heights Plastic Surgery? In Houston Brazilian Butt Lift is an outpatient procedure that typically takes about 2 to 3 hours to complete. To begin, a general anesthetic will be administered to ensure you are comfortable and pain-free throughout your surgery. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will then perform gentle liposuction to remove fat from the chosen donor areas of the body, typically the back, flanks, arms, or abdomen. The harvested fat is collected and purified to ensure only healthy tissue and cells are used for transfer into the buttocks. The fat is then strategically and carefully injected into areas of the buttocks that require lift and reshaping. Butt implants may be used at this time to enhance the results of your Brazilian Butt Lift procedure. What does recovery after a Brazilian butt lift look like? For the first 24 hours following your Brazilian Butt Lift Houston patients may experience some pain, soreness, swelling, and bruising around your treated areas. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will provide you with prescription medication that can help manage your pain. You will also be asked to wear a surgical compression garment to reduce swelling and promote proper healing. It is important that you refrain from sitting on your bottom or lay on your back for two to three weeks following your Brazilian Butt Lift. Doing so may compromise your expected results. Patients typically report returning to work and normal daily activities after about four weeks following their Brazilian Butt Lift treatment; however, it is important to wait until given clearance from Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks.
