Beautiful lips Lip Augmentation Lip Augmentation Methods Restylane injections are a non-permanent, non-surgical lip augmentation method that generally lasts four and up to twelve months. Please visit our Restylane page to learn more. The fat injection method is a semi-permanent, non-surgical method that uses fat harvested from the patient’s body. The dermal fat graft method is a semi-permanent surgical lip augmentation method that uses fat attached to deep layers of skin, which is harvested from the patient’s body. The V-Y lip advancement method is a permanent surgical lip augmentation method. As the incision heals, the lips expand to create fullness.
