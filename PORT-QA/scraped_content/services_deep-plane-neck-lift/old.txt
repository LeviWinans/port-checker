A Deep Plane Neck Lift is a surgical procedure that improves the contour of the neck and jawline by addressing deeper structures beneath the skin, including the platysma muscle, excess fat, and tissue laxity. Rather than relying on skin tightening alone, this technique is designed to create a more structural, natural-looking improvement in the neck. For the right patient, this matters. The neck is not just skin. Aging in this area often involves separated platysmal bands, deep fullness, muscle laxity, and skin that no longer drapes cleanly beneath the chin. A Deep Plane Neck Lift is meant to correct that more comprehensively. In some patients, it is performed as a standalone procedure. In others, it is paired with a Deep Plane Facelift when the jawline, lower face, and neck all need to be treated as one connected unit. What aesthetic issues does a Deep Plane Neck Lift address? This procedure is designed for patients whose concerns go beyond a little looseness. With a Deep Plane Neck Lift Houston patients may address: Visible neck bands Loose or crepey neck skin Fullness beneath the chin A soft or poorly defined jawline Tissue descent beneath the jaw A neck that looks older than the face Persistent heaviness that does not improve with weight loss The neck can age in layers. Skin quality is one part of it. Platysmal separation is another. Fat under the chin may also contribute, that is either above or below the platysma muscle.. When several of those issues show up together, a deeper neck approach often makes more sense than a limited lift. What is a Deep Plane Neck Lift vs. a standard neck lift? Not every neck lift is the same. A standard neck lift may focus on removing excess skin, addressing visible banding, and improving contour through more limited dissection. That can work beautifully for the right patient. A Deep Plane Neck Lift goes further. It is designed for patients who need deeper structural correction through the neck, especially when the platysma, submental fullness, and jawline relationship all need more precise treatment. It is a more advanced approach, and it is typically chosen when anatomy calls for more than a lighter lift. The point is not to choose the most extensive procedure. The point is to choose the one that actually fits the neck. Deep Plane Neck Lift benefits A Deep Plane Neck Lift offers a different quality of correction than surface tightening alone. The change is often more convincing because the neck is being reshaped at the structural level. Benefits of a Deep Plane Neck Lift may include: A sharper jawline-neck transition Smoother neck contour Improvement in platysmal banding Better definition beneath the chin Removal of excess skin when needed More natural-looking correction without a pulled appearance Longer-lasting improvement compared with lighter neck-tightening approaches A result that better matches facial rejuvenation procedures when combined For many patients, the biggest benefit is proportion. The neck stops competing with the face. Related Procedures

The Procedure A Deep Plane Neck Lift is typically performed as an outpatient procedure under general anesthesia or IV sedation, depending on the surgical plan. Incisions are usually placed around the ear and, when needed, in a discreet location beneath the chin. Once access is gained, the deeper neck structures are addressed directly. This may involve tightening or reapproximating the platysma muscle, removing excess fat underneath the platysma muscle, refining muscles underneath the platysma muscle, refining submental fullness, and redraping the skin without relying on excessive tension. That distinction matters. The best deep plane neck lift results do not come from simply pulling skin tighter. They come from restoring better support underneath it. For some patients, targeted neck liposuction is the procedure of choice. For others, a neck lift or a deep neck lift with the deeper correction is what makes the difference. Longer-term settling The neck continues to refine over several weeks and months. The final result is not immediate. It develops as the tissues soften and settle into their new position. Deep Plane Neck Lift vs. Non-surgical Treatments Nonsurgical treatments can improve skin quality, texture, and mild laxity. They can be useful maintenance tools. They cannot recreate the structural correction of a surgical neck lift. If the main issue is deep banding, substantial looseness, or a heavy under-chin contour, surgery is usually the more accurate answer. A Deep Plane Neck Lift is not a substitute for skincare or devices. It is the next step when those options are no longer enough. Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About Deep Plane Neck Lift in Houston What is the difference between a Deep Plane Neck Lift and a regular neck lift? A Deep Plane Neck Lift addresses deeper structures of the neck more extensively. It is often better for patients with more advanced banding, skin laxity, or structural fullness. Is a Deep Plane Neck Lift always combined with a facelift? No. Some patients need only neck correction. However, it is typically combined with a facelift when the lower face also shows signs of aging. Will a Deep Plane Neck Lift get rid of neck bands? It is one of the better surgical options for visible platysmal banding, especially when those bands are part of a broader structural aging pattern. How long is recovery? Most patients need at least one to two weeks of visible recovery, with additional settling over the following weeks. Will I have a scar under my chin? Some patients do. When needed, the incision is small and placed in a discreet location to help refine the contour beneath the chin. How long do results last? Results are long-lasting, though aging continues. Longevity depends on anatomy, skin quality, sun exposure, and whether the procedure is paired with other facial rejuvenation. Am I too young for a Deep Plane Neck Lift? Candidacy depends on anatomy, not a birthday. Some patients develop neck heaviness or banding earlier and may still be good candidates.
