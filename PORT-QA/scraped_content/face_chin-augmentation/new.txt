A Well-defined Chin Chin Augmentation A well-defined chin may not be your first aesthetic concern, but perhaps it should be. A receding chin can make the nose and upper teeth appear more prominent and make the neck appear shorter. Chin augmentation surgery is a relatively simple procedure that lengthens the chin, balancing the cheeks and nose and elongating the appearance of the neck. The procedure is performed in our office, usually on an outpatient basis. Chin augmentation is typically performed with local anesthesia and sedation.
