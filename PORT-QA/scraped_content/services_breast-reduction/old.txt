Breast Reconstruction What is a Breast Reduction Procedure? A breast reduction procedure (reduction mammaplasty) is a complex surgical intervention that removes excess fat, excess skin, and excess glandular tissue from overly large breasts. During the operation, the surgeon physically elevates the nipple-areolar complex to a higher position on the chest wall, creating a lighter, highly proportionate breast contour. What Physical and Aesthetic Issues Does It Address? Heavy breasts dictate how a woman moves, sleeps, and lives. We utilize this surgery to correct both the internal structural strain and the external visual distortion caused by excess breast size. Most women carrying this weight experience a cascading series of physical failures. Cervical and Thoracic Strain: The sheer downward pull of a heavy chest causes the upper spine to curve forward. Removing this weight immediately relieves chronic pain in the neck and shoulders. Shoulder Grooving: Constant tension from bra straps cuts into the trapezius muscle, causing nerve compression and permanent indentations. Chronic Rashes: Skin-on-skin friction beneath a heavy, pendulous breast causes constant dermatitis. Elevating the tissue clears the breast crease completely. Ptosis (Sagging): Weight destroys skin elasticity. We excise the redundant, stretched skin and move the nipple from pointing downward to a forward-facing, centered position. The Physical and Emotional Benefits of Structural Reduction The primary advantage of a breast reduction operation is the immediate, profound improvement in physical mechanics. The cosmetic refinement is secondary to the functional relief. You wake up from the surgery physically lighter. Unrestricted Movement: You can finally run, jump, and perform physical activities without double-layering sports bras. You experience significantly less pain during exertion. Psychological Relief: Many women feel self-conscious about their chest from a very young age. This deep self-consciousness limits their clothing choices and social interactions. Eliminating this burden resolves years of emotional discomfort. Clothing Fit: You can buy structured tops, dresses, and swimwear off the rack without sizing up strictly to accommodate your chest volume.

Candidates for Breast Reduction in Houston Surgical success relies on correct patient selection. We evaluate your anatomy to ensure you are a safe candidate for extensive tissue excision. Houston breast reduction patients must meet specific clinical criteria. Symptomatic Patients: You experience documented physical discomfort, numbness in the arms, or chronic skin issues directly related to your large breasts. Stable Body Weight: Significant weight fluctuations after surgery will alter your new breast contour. You must be at a maintainable baseline weight. Comprehensive Review: During your initial consultation, we meticulously review your complete medical history. Nicotine Abstinence: Nicotine drastically constricts blood vessels, leading to tissue death. You must be entirely free of all nicotine products for six weeks prior to any plastic surgery. The Surgical Procedure We perform breast reductions in our private, fully accredited surgical facility. Ensuring optimal patient comfort, you are placed under general anesthesia by a board-certified anesthesiologist. Your surgeon executes the specific surgical technique discussed during your consultation. We carefully preserve the blood supply and nerve pathways to maintain nipple sensation while aggressively excising the dense, heavy lower pole of the breast tissue. We remove excess fat and reshape the remaining tissue into a tight, elevated mound. The nipple-areolar complex is shifted upward to the apex of the new breast. We close the incisions with deep, layered sutures to support the new internal architecture. Combining Procedures In plastic surgery, functional restoration often overlaps with aesthetic refinement. While some patients strictly require a reduction, others integrate additional operations into a single recovery period. The Mommy Makeover: We frequently combine breast reduction surgery with a tummy tuck to completely repair the abdominal wall after pregnancy. Volume Adjustments: A breast reduction subtracts tissue. Conversely, patients who have lost massive amounts of weight may require an isolated breast lift to elevate empty skin, or a breast augmentation surgery using breast implants to restore lost upper pole fullness. Total Body Harmony: While our practice is globally recognized for complex reconstructive surgery and facial rejuvenation, body contouring procedures demand their own dedicated structural engineering. We ensure your upper body perfectly matches your lower body. Expected Results The relief from the skeletal weight is instantaneous. However, the visual final results require patience. Initially, the breasts will appear very high and tight on the chest wall. Over the first few months, the tissue undergoes a physical settling process. The skin relaxes, and the breast settles into a soft, natural teardrop shape. To achieve optimal results, you must allow the internal sutures time to dissolve and the tissue to soften. The scars will be firm and red initially, slowly fading into flat, pale lines. The Healing Process and Timeline Healing from a structural reduction is a highly managed physical progression. We guide you through every phase to ensure proper healing. Days 1–3: You will feel tight and fatigued. You must sleep rigidly on your back. We prescribe specific pain medicine to manage early discomfort easily. You will transition off heavy pain medication quickly. Several Weeks Out: Most patients drive a car and return to light desk work within ten days. We utilize specialized garments to actively reduce swelling. Four to Six Weeks: The primary swelling subsides. The incisions begin to seal completely. Many patients resume moderate cardiovascular exercise. Clinical Aftercare Your actions during the breast reduction recovery window dictate the quality of your scars and the longevity of your contour. Strict Compression: You must wear your surgical compression bra consistently. It physically forces the empty tissue spaces to collapse and supports the delicate internal repairs. Activity Restrictions: You must strictly avoid heavy lifting and vigorous exercise for a minimum of six weeks. Elevating your heart rate prematurely invites bleeding and structural failure. Scar Management: Once the incisions are fully closed, we initiate a strict scar protocol using medical-grade silicone to flatten the incision lines.

Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About Breast Reductions in Houston What is a breast reduction? Women who suffer from overly large breasts often experience emotional and physical distress that can make normal, everyday activities very difficult. In Houston breast reduction is a popular cosmetic surgery that removes excess skin and glandular tissue from the breasts to create smaller breasts that better suit the patient’s frame and lifestyle. Houston board-certified plastic surgeons, Dr. Cara Downey and Dr. Jamal Bullocks, have performed hundreds of breast reduction surgeries throughout their career. The results of a breast reduction are long-lasting, have the potential to be permanent, and leave women with newfound physical and emotional comfort. How much does a breast reduction cost in Houston? The average cost of a breast reduction in Houston typically ranges from $6,500 to $10,000. The total cost for your breast reduction will depend on factors such as your surgeon’s fee, the location of your procedure, and other miscellaneous medical expenses. At Heights Plastic Surgery, we accept cash and all major credit cards. We are also happy to offer financing through CareCredit® for patients. Breast reduction may be considered medically necessary based on the volume of the breast and associated symptoms. It is not covered by all insurance plans. How do I prepare for a breast reduction? To prepare for your breast reduction, Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks suggests a healthy diet in the weeks leading up to surgery is a must. Maintaining a healthy diet will aid in your recovery process post-surgery. It is also important to refrain from smoking for about four to eight weeks before and after surgery to avoid the risk of complications both during and post-surgery. How does Heights Plastic Surgery perform a breast reduction? A breast reduction is an outpatient procedure that typically takes about two to four hours to complete. To begin, general anesthesia will be administered to ensure you are comfortable and pain-free throughout your surgery. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will reduce the overall size of your breast and manipulate its position to look more natural on your frame. The nipple is typically separated from the outer skin but left attached to its blood supply within the breast tissue in order to position it higher on the chest. In large reductions with nipples set very far from your chest wall, removal and replacement of the nipple may be indicated. After incisions are closed, the surgeon will protect the treatment areas with a supportive bra and surgical dressings. What techniques do you offer for a breast reduction? Houston breast reduction experts, Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks may use any of the following techniques to enhance and re-contour your breasts during your breast reduction: Inverted-T (Anchor). This technique removes a large amount of tissue and skin to drastically reduce the size of the breasts, resulting in an anchor-shaped scar. Both the nipples and areola are repositioned in order to complement your new breast shape. Vertical (Lollipop). This technique is typically used to remove a slight-to-moderate volume of breast tissue. The resulting scar runs around the areola then towards the breast crease, mimicking the shape of a lollipop. Liposuction Breast Reduction. Liposuction may be used as a standalone procedure to receive a very slight reduction in breast size. It is often used in conjunction with the previous techniques to remove fat from the top or sides of the breast, creating a rounder, perkier, and more natural shape. What does recovery look like after a breast reduction? For the first 24 hours following your breast reduction from Heights Plastic Surgery, it is normal to experience moderate pain in the chest and nausea as a result of the anesthesia used during surgery. Your surgical dressings and surgical bra may be removed after 48 hours of surgery. After about two to three days following your breast reduction, you may resume showering as normal. We recommend avoiding the use of narcotic pain medication after three days. Within 1 to 2 weeks, you may return back to work and resume low-impact daily activities and exercises provided your breasts are supported. Driving is typically not recommended until it is no longer painful to wear a seatbelt. Around 6 to 8 weeks after your breast reduction, you may resume normal activities and workout routines without hesitation. Within about three months following your procedure, your breast reduction scars will begin to fade and your breasts’ final shape will begin to settle. Am I a good candidate for a breast reduction in Houston? Good candidates for breast reduction are patients who are in good general health. Women above the age of 40 or those who carry a significant family history of breast cancer are required to have a mammogram screening prior to surgery. Additionally, women who experience irregularities such as nipple discharge, skin dimpling, or lumps also need to undergo screening tests to ensure their safety.
