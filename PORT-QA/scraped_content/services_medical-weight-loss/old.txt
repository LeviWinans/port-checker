Semaglutide: Your Appetite’s New BFF For our patients in Houston Semaglutide can be a powerful addition to your weight loss routine. This injectable medication works by mimicking a natural hormone in your body called glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1). GLP-1 helps regulate appetite and digestion, and Semaglutide gives it a boost. This can lead to an increased feeling of fullness that lasts longer. As a result, you may experience reduced cravings and potentially consume fewer calories overall. Tirzepatide: A Double Whammy for Weight Loss Tirzepatide can be viewed as an advanced development of Semaglutide. It’s still under FDA review for weight management, but early research is promising. Similar to Semaglutide, it targets the GLP-1 hormone, which influences satiety and blood sugar control. However, Tirzepatide takes an additional step by also impacting glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP), another hormone involved in appetite and digestion. This dual approach has the potential to lead to even greater weight loss compared to Semaglutide alone. Why Choose a Medical Weight Loss Management Program for Lasting Results? How do I know which weight loss procedure is right for me? In your consultation for medical weight loss Houston providers evaluate you and measure your BMI, body fat percentage and metabolic function. Only then, can we decide which procedure or procedures will help you meet your goals. What is Semaglutide? One of the options we provide is an injectable treatment called Semaglutide. Semaglutide is a FDA approved weight loss drug for those with a BMI over 30 or over 27 with a comorbidity. It targets the brain to decrease cravings and appetite, and also works to slow down the gut to decrease hunger. In conjunction with lifestyle changes of improved nutrition and regular exercise, you can see changes in just a few weeks. The drug is administered weekly in the form of an injection and the dose is increased depending on responsiveness and side effects. Typically, doses are altered monthly. A follow up is required before any change in dosage to monitor side effects and weight. What is Tirzepatide and how does it work for weight loss? Tirzepatide is a prescription injectable medication for chronic weight management. Similar to Semaglutide, it targets hormones that regulate appetite and gut motility, promoting feelings of fullness and reducing hunger. Studies suggest that Tirzepatide may be even more effective for weight loss compared to Semaglutide. What are the potential benefits of Semaglutide and Tirzepatide for weight loss? Both medications, alongside lifestyle changes, can lead to significant weight loss. Potential benefits include: Reduced body weight and BMI Improved blood sugar control Lower risk of developing type 2 diabetes (for those at risk) Improved cardiovascular health (ongoing research) What are some common side effects of Semaglutide and Tirzepatide? The most common side effects are usually mild to moderate and may improve over time. These can include: Nausea Vomiting Diarrhea Constipation Stomach pain How much do Semaglutide and Tirzepatide cost, and does insurance cover them?
