Non-Surgical. Immediate. Long-lasting. Silhouette InstaLift® uses an innovative Micro-Suspension Technology™ to lift and add volume to mid-facial skin. Micro-cones are placed in the deepest layer of the skin. These bi-directional cones provide an immediate and long-lasting lift. The micro-cones and sutures are absorbed by the body and activate your natural collagen production over time for up to 2 years, for a more youthful appearance. The InstaLift procedure takes place right in our office and is done in less than an hour. Plus, you’ll walk out with an immediate lift thanks to Micro-suspension Technology™. And, over time, you’ll continue to see results as InstaLift boosts your natural collagen production, adding volume to your cheeks and mid-face. These results may be long lasting. To learn more about the Silhouette InstaLift in Bend, Oregon, please contact the office of Dual Board Certified Facial Plastic Surgeon Dr. Michael Villano at VillanoMD to schedule your consultation and determine if this treatment is right for you!
