The Silhouette Instalift at VillanoMD The Silhouette Instalift at VillanoMD Silhouette InstaLift® uses an innovative Micro-Suspension Technology™ to lift and add volume to mid-facial skin. Micro-cones are placed in the deepest layer of the skin. These bi-directional cones provide an immediate and long-lasting lift. The micro-cones and sutures are absorbed by the body and activate your natural collagen production over time for up to 2 years, for a more youthful appearance. Non-Surgical. Immediate. Long-lasting. The InstaLift procedure takes place right in our office and is done in less than an hour. Plus, you’ll walk out with an immediate lift thanks to Micro-suspension Technology™. And, over time, you’ll continue to see results as InstaLift boosts your natural collagen production, adding volume to your cheeks and mid-face. These results may be long lasting.
