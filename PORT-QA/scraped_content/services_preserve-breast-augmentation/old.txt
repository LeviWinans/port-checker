Preservé breast augmentation uses proprietary Motiva instruments to create an implant pocket while minimizing disruption of native support structures. A Motiva SmoothSilk® Ergonomix® implant is placed for soft, natural-looking outcomes. Controlled tissue expansion forms space for the implant while preserving natural tissue, support structures, sensation, and the pectoral muscle when anatomy allows. Local anesthesia and light sedation let qualified patients avoid general anesthesia. How Does the Preservé Technique Work? Through a small incision, specialized instruments create a precise channel and gently expand the breast tissue. A dedicated sleeve supports controlled implant placement with limited handling. Local anesthesia numbs the area while light sedation promotes relaxation. Preserving the pectoral muscle and using this lower-impact approach may mean less tissue trauma and an easier recovery. Experiences vary. Preservé vs. Traditional Breast Augmentation Traditional augmentation often uses general anesthesia and more extensive pocket dissection. It remains valuable for thin tissue coverage, significant asymmetry, prior surgery, or specific implant goals. Preservé breast augmentation takes a more conservative approach. Breast tissue, desired size, skin quality, and lifestyle determine which technique offers the safest balance. Why Avoiding General Anesthesia Matters For some women, anesthesia is the concern. With Preservé breast augmentation Houston patients who are qualified have the option to avoid the loss of consciousness, airway support, and deeper medications associated with general anesthesia. Local anesthesia controls sensation at the surgical site, while light sedation supports calm and comfort. This approach may also reduce anesthesia-related concerns such as postoperative nausea, mental fogginess, or a sore throat. It does not remove the need for monitoring. Related Procedures

Recovery After Preservé Breast Augmentation Following Preservé breast augmentation Houston patients can expect tightness, swelling, bruising, and temporary soreness. Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About Preservé Breast Augmentation in Houston Can Preservé breast augmentation really be performed without general anesthesia? Yes. Local anesthesia and light sedation can keep qualified patients comfortable without general anesthesia. Dr. Downey reviews your health, anxiety, anatomy, and surgical plan before confirming the safest approach. Is Preservé breast augmentation less painful? Minimizing disruption may reduce discomfort for some patients. Pain still varies with anatomy, implant size, and healing. Does Preservé include a breast lift? No. Augmentation adds volume; a breast lift repositions sagging tissue. Selected patients may combine them. How large can I go with Preservé? Size depends on tissue coverage, breast width, skin quality, and safety. Your surgeon will recommend dimensions that respect your anatomy. Can Preservé prevent implant complications? No. It cannot eliminate infection, capsular contracture, rupture, malposition, sensation changes, or future surgery. How long do the results last? Results can last years, but implants are not lifetime devices. Regular follow-up and imaging remain essential.
