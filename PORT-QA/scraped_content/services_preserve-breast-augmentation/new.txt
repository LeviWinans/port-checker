Proportion, Softness, and Natural Movement. Am i a Candidate? Preservé breast augmentation offers Houston patients an advanced option designed around proportion, softness, and natural movement. At Heights Plastic Surgery, Preservé by Motiva® minimizes disruption to natural breast tissue and the pectoral muscle. It can be performed without general anesthesia, an important distinction for women uneasy about being fully unconscious. Procedure Overview Augmentation Mammoplasty also known as: breast augmentation \ og-men-TAY-shun MA-moh-pla-stee \ Plastic surgery procedure that adds volume and symmetry to the breasts via breast implant or fat grafting Procedure options: Breast Augmentation, Silicone Breast Implant Enhancement, Transaxillary Breast Augmentation, Peri-areolar Breast Augmentation, Breast Augmentation Lift Addresses Naturally small breasts, Mild breast asymmetry, Volume loss after pregnancy, aging, or weight fluctuations, Desire for subtle, proportional enhancement Procedure Time 1–2 hours Eligibility Female, 22+, Non-smoker, At least 6 months post-breastfeeding, Adequate natural breast tissue and skin quality Possible Side Effects Swelling, Bruising, Temporary tightness or discomfort, Temporary changes to breast or nipple sensation What Is Preservé Breast Augmentation? Preservé breast augmentation uses proprietary Motiva instruments to create an implant pocket while minimizing disruption of native support structures. A Motiva SmoothSilk® Ergonomix® implant is placed for soft, natural-looking outcomes. Controlled tissue expansion forms space for the implant while preserving natural tissue, support structures, sensation, and the pectoral muscle when anatomy allows. Local anesthesia and light sedation let qualified patients avoid general anesthesia.

How Does the Preservé Technique Work? Through a small incision, specialized instruments create a precise channel and gently expand the breast tissue. A dedicated sleeve supports controlled implant placement with limited handling. Local anesthesia numbs the area while light sedation promotes relaxation. Preserving the pectoral muscle and using this lower-impact approach may mean less tissue trauma and an easier recovery. Experiences vary.

Preservé vs. Traditional Breast Augmentation Traditional augmentation often uses general anesthesia and more extensive pocket dissection. It remains valuable for thin tissue coverage, significant asymmetry, prior surgery, or specific implant goals. Preservé breast augmentation takes a more conservative approach. Breast tissue, desired size, skin quality, and lifestyle determine which technique offers the safest balance.

Why Avoiding General Anesthesia Matters Schedule Today For some women, anesthesia is the concern. With Preservé breast augmentation Houston patients who are qualified have the option to avoid the loss of consciousness, airway support, and deeper medications associated with general anesthesia. Local anesthesia controls sensation at the surgical site, while light sedation supports calm and comfort. This approach may also reduce anesthesia-related concerns such as postoperative nausea, mental fogginess, or a sore throat. It does not remove the need for monitoring. Breast Reconstruction Am I a Candidate for Preservé Breast Augmentation? Good candidates are healthy women seeking primary breast augmentation with enough tissue to support the implant. Preservé breast augmentation may appeal to patients whose concerns about general anesthesia have delayed surgery. They must still qualify for local anesthesia and sedation. Significant loose skin, marked drooping, limited tissue coverage, or complex revision needs may require traditional augmentation, a breast lift, fat transfer, or combined surgery. Pregnancy, aging, and weight fluctuations can change the result.

Recovery After Preservé Breast Augmentation Following Preservé breast augmentation Houston patients can expect tightness, swelling, bruising, and temporary soreness. Avoiding general anesthesia may mean less nausea, grogginess, or throat discomfort, although responses vary. Many patients resume light activity relatively quickly. Driving, work, exercise, lifting, and upper-body movement restart with the surgeon’s approval. Recovery varies with anatomy, implant size, and additional procedures. Contact Heights Plastic Surgery promptly for increasing pain, one-sided swelling, fever, drainage, shortness of breath, or another unexpected change.

Preservé Breast Augmentation Results The breasts initially sit higher and feel firmer. Their final shape develops as swelling resolves and the implants settle. Results can be long-lasting, but breast implants are not lifetime devices. Aging, pregnancy, gravity, and weight fluctuations continue to affect natural tissue. Future imaging, implant exchange, revision, or a breast lift may eventually be needed.

Risks and Breast Implant Safety Schedule Today Possible complications include bleeding, infection, delayed healing, scarring, asymmetry, sensation changes, malposition, rippling, rupture, capsular contracture, pain, and additional surgery. Local anesthesia and sedation also carry risks. Motiva SmoothSilk Round and Round Ergonomix silicone implants are FDA-approved for breast augmentation in women age 22 and older. FDA approval applies to the implant; Preservé describes the specialized technique and instruments used to place it. Review the manufacturer’s decision checklist before surgery. The FDA recommends ultrasound or MRI screening for silent rupture after five to six years and every two to three years afterward. Preservé Breast Augmentation Frequently Asked Questions Can Preservé breast augmentation really be performed without general anesthesia? Yes. Local anesthesia and light sedation can keep qualified patients comfortable without general anesthesia. Dr. Downey reviews your health, anxiety, anatomy, and surgical plan before confirming the safest approach. Is Preservé breast augmentation less painful? Minimizing disruption may reduce discomfort for some patients. Pain still varies with anatomy, implant size, and healing. Does Preservé include a breast lift? No. Augmentation adds volume; a breast lift repositions sagging tissue. Selected patients may combine them. How large can I go with Preservé? Size depends on tissue coverage, breast width, skin quality, and safety. Your surgeon will recommend dimensions that respect your anatomy. Can Preservé prevent implant complications? No. It cannot eliminate infection, capsular contracture, rupture, malposition, sensation changes, or future surgery. How long do the results last? Results can last years, but implants are not lifetime devices. Regular follow-up and imaging remain essential.
