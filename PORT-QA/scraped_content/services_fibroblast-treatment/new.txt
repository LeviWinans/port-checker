Fibroblast Treatment Frequently Asked Questions What is fibroblast treatment? Our fibroblast treatment that we offer is an innovative, non-invasive, skin lifting, skin tightening and rejuvenation device designed to combat wrinkles, loss of collagen and sagging skin. The device we carry is FDA approved and can be used in a variety of procedures to potentially enhance aging skin without the need for expensive and invasive surgery. In Houston fibroblast treatment is used to enhance skin around the eyes, cheeks, neck, forehead, lips, stomach, and many more areas. How much does fibroblast treatment cost in Houston? The average cost of a fibroblast treatment in Houston typically ranges from $850-$3000. The total cost for your fibroblast treatment will depend on factors including the location of your procedure. At Studio Essex, we accept cash and all major credit cards. We are also happy to offer financing through CareCredit® for patients. What should I expect after a fibroblasting treatment? Our fibroblast skin treatment is a minimally invasive procedure that causes superficial scab-like injuries on the treated area that naturally flake off within a week. Following fibroblast treatments Houston patients experience their skin being swollen and sensitive for the next few days. This is normal and typically resolves on its own. Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks advise you to moisturize the treated area with the topical serum that is given to you in the office to ensure a smooth and swift recovery. While many patients may start to see the results of their fibroblast treatment within a few days, it takes up to 12 weeks to see the ultimate results. Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks recommend combining this treatment with other procedures such as Botox, d, and/or chemical peels. Am I a good candidate for fibroblasting? Good candidates for fibroblasting treatments with our device are in good general health and have realistic expectations of the results they can expect from their treatment. Ideal candidates for fibroblasting often desire to treat the following concerns: Fine lines and wrinkles Mild sagging facial skin- most often around the eyes, jowls, neck Hooded eyes Acne scarring, stretch marks Milia Before a fibroblasting treatment in Houston at Studio Essex, we instruct all patients to discontinue retinol, Vitamin C, and any active acids starting 3 days prior to their treatment.
