View All Before & Afters Controlled Exfoliation What is a HydraFacial? At our practice in Houston HydraFacial is a three-step system that uses a patented handpiece to exfoliate, extract, and infuse in one continuous flow. First, controlled exfoliation loosens dead skin cells on the skin’s surface without the abrasion of older facials. Next, a light glycolic/salicylic blend softens impurities inside the pores. Then vortex suction clears oil, blackheads, and debris while serums rich in hyaluronic acid, peptides, and antioxidants are infused to hydrate and protect. It’s efficient, mechanical in the best way, and more consistent than manual facials. No harsh scrubbing, no guessing when to stop. Just measured resurfacing that respects the barrier.
