What is a Neck Lift? A neck lift, also called a lower rhytidectomy, is a surgical procedure that refines the contour of the neck and jawline by removing excess skin and excess fat, tightening the platysma muscle, and redraping remaining skin for a smooth, natural-looking result. At Heights Plastic Surgery in Houston, board-certified plastic surgeons Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks tailor the neck lift procedure to each patient’s anatomy, using limited incision techniques, cervicoplasty, platysmaplasty, and, when useful, targeted neck liposuction. The goal is neck rejuvenation that restores balance between the face and neck without the “pulled tight” look.

What Aesthetic Issues Does a Neck Lift Address? The neck often shows visible signs of aging sooner than the face due to skin laxity, changes in the underlying neck muscles, and fat accumulation under the chin. A neck lift addresses sagging skin, drooping jowls along the jawline, neck bands created by platysma separation, and fullness that reads as a double chin. It also improves creases related to sun damage from prolonged sun exposure and helps harmonize facial features so the lower face and neck look refreshed and proportional.

Neck Lift Procedure Neck lift surgery is typically an outpatient procedure performed under general anesthesia, though select cases may use local anesthesia with sedation. After carefully planned markings, small incisions are placed around the ear and sometimes beneath the chin. When excess fat is present, the surgeon may remove it through neck liposuction. The platysma muscle is tightened to correct neck bands and reinforce support, then remaining skin is redraped to refine the neck area without tension. Incisions are closed with fine sutures, and a light compression bandage is placed to limit swelling and protect the incision site. The operation usually takes two to three hours, depending on whether other procedures such as a brow lift, mini facelift, or fat contouring are done the same day.

Recovery Schedule Today Neck lift recovery is straightforward for most patients. Expect mild swelling, bruising, and a sense of tightness for the first few days. Keeping the head elevated, even during sleep, reduces pressure and speeds the recovery process. Light walking is encouraged early, but strenuous activity and heavy lifting should wait until cleared by your surgeon. A compression bandage is typically worn as directed to control swelling and support deeper structures. Most people resume normal activities and return to work within ten to fourteen days, once bruising fades and comfort returns. Numbness along the incision line is common and improves as the nerves settle. Sun avoidance and daily sunscreen are essential to protect healing skin and minimize visible scarring. Aftercare Incision care is simple and guided by your team. Gentle cleansing, prescribed ointments, and later transition to silicone scar care help incisions mature into fine, easily concealed lines. Avoiding nicotine, alcohol excess, and unnecessary blood thinners supports clean healing. Patients are advised to limit wide neck rotation early on and to follow a gradual return to exercise. Ongoing skin health maintenance using topical antioxidants, sunscreen, and physician-grade skincare helps maintain results. Studio Essex, the medspa within Heights Plastic Surgery, offers complementary options such as targeted radiofrequency tightening, neuromodulators for platysma bands when appropriate, and pigment care for sun damage, all of which can extend the longevity of a surgical result.
