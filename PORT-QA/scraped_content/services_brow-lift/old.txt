A brow lift, sometimes called a forehead lift, is a procedure that gently raises the brows and smooths out the forehead. At Heights Plastic Surgery, we use techniques like small-incision (endoscopic) lifts or direct lifts, depending on what’s best for you. During the procedure, your surgeon repositions the tissues, tightens the muscles, and removes any extra skin if needed. The goal is to create a refreshed, natural look that still feels like you—just more awake and rested. What Aesthetic Issues Does a Brow Lift Address? A brow lift is a great option if your brows sit lower than you’d like or if you’re noticing deeper lines across your forehead or between your brows. It can help with sagging skin that creates a tired or even frustrated expression. Uneven brows—where one sits lower than the other—can also be corrected during this procedure. Whether it’s about softening lines, evening out your brow position, or giving your upper face a bit of a lift, this treatment can help you look more refreshed and relaxed. How to Prepare for a Brow Lift Before your procedure, you’ll want to pause smoking or vaping a few weeks ahead of time to support healing. We’ll review all your medications and supplements—some may need to be stopped temporarily. You’ll also need to set up a calm space at home to rest, with soft pillows and easy meals ready. And be sure to have someone lined up to drive you home afterward. A little planning goes a long way in making recovery smooth and comfortable. The Brow Lift Procedure On the day of your brow lift surgery, we’ll start with either a local numbing agent or general anesthesia, depending on your plan and comfort. The surgeon then makes small incisions, repositions the underlying tissues, and tightens or smooths the area to create a more lifted look. Extra skin, if needed, is gently removed. The procedure usually takes about one to two hours, and once you’re fully awake and feeling good, you’ll be able to head home that same day. Brow Lift Results You’ll start to notice changes in the first couple of weeks, especially as swelling starts to go down. Most Houston brow lift patients see the full results around the two-to-three-month mark, once everything has fully settled. The results can last for several years, especially when paired with healthy skin habits and sun protection. We’ll walk you through what to expect and how to keep that refreshed look going strong. Brow Lift Recovery Recovery after a brow lift is usually straightforward. Some swelling, tightness, or mild bruising is normal right after surgery, but it tends to ease up quickly. We’ll ask you to keep your head elevated and take it easy for the first few days. Most people are back to light daily activities within a week, though we recommend holding off on intense exercise for a few weeks longer. We’ll give you specific guidelines to make sure everything heals smoothly. Brow Lift Aftercare Once you’re healed, simple steps can help extend your results. Sunscreen is key—protecting your skin from the sun helps keep everything looking smooth and even. Gentle skincare also makes a difference, and we may suggest treatments down the road to maintain or enhance your results. Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About Brow Lifts in Houston What is a brow lift? A brow lift, sometimes referred to as a “forehead lift,” surgically lifts the soft tissue and skin of the forehead to improve the appearance of the brow and/or eyes. This can provide much more compelling, long-term results for patients struggling with moderate to severe sagging. Oftentimes, brow lift patients already have experience with using cosmetic injectables (like Botox or Filler) and other non-surgical options, but the laxity of their skin has reached a point where these treatments are no longer effective in fully addressing their concerns. To make the most of our patients’ time and investment, the surgeons at HPS often perform brow lifts in conjunction with other facial surgery techniques, such as a mini facelift, blepharoplasty, or other procedures that require general anesthesia. What brow lift techniques do you offer at Heights Plastic Surgery? Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks are highly experienced in the six types of brow lifts in Houston: Coronal Lift. A coronal lift is a technical term for a “classic” brow lift. During a coronal brow lift, we place an incision just above the ear and extend this through the hair to lift the skin and remove any excess. Endoscopic Brow Lift. During an endoscopic brow lift in Houston, we create small incisions hidden in the hair and use specialized equipment to gently release and elevate the brow tissue. Because we consider this “minimally invasive,” this technique can improve healing and downtime compared to a traditional brow lift. Temporal Brow Lift. For younger patients who would still like to see more dramatic improvement to their forehead and brow, a temporal brow lift may be an ideal option. In this technique, you are not required to go under general anesthesia. Instead, we use a local anesthetic to perform a much more scaled-back version of a traditional brow lift. Direct Brow Lift. During a direct brow lift, typically for older patients, we may place the incision right at the brow to help correct deep wrinkles and excess skin. Transblepharoplasty. Particularly popular with male patients, as transblepharoplasty is an “add on” procedure to an eyelid lift that simultaneously allows us to make small adjustments to the brow. Pretrichial Brow Lift. For patients with long foreheads, this brow lift technique is effective for not only lifting the brow, but also reducing the overall size and length of the forehead for a more balanced appearance.
