Beauty Enhancer Restylane Treatments in Bend Oregon Restylane is a clear gel that is injected into or just under the skin in small amounts to restore volume and structure. It utilizes hyaluronic acid similar to the body’s own hyaluronic acid. The hyaluronic acid in Restylane is non-animal based, which means no skin tests are needed and the risk of allergic or hypersensitive reactions is minimal. Restylane is one of the safest aesthetic treatments available today. It is a non-surgical procedure and requires minimal downtime. Since its launch in 1996, millions have enjoyed the benefits of Restylane, making it one of the world’s most widely used hyaluronic acid-based aesthetic injection treatments.
