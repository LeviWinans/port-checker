Enhancing Your Natural Features with a Lower Body Lift You’ve put in the work, but loose, sagging skin can still hold you back from fully enjoying your results. A lower body lift is designed to remove excess skin, refine your contours, and help your body finally reflect how far you’ve come. At Heights Plastic Surgery, our board-certified surgeons provide a personalized approach to help you feel confident, comfortable, and proud in your own skin. Body Lift Overview Belt Lipectomy, also known as lower body lift is a plastic surgery procedure that removes excess skin and fat from the lower half of the body, commonly performed after significant weight loss. The procedure includes abdominoplasty and thigh and buttock lifting by removing a circumferential composite of tissue from the trunk. Address: Abdomen, Waist, Pubic Area, Outer Thighs, Buttocks, Flanks/Hips, Lower Back Procedure Time: 4 - 8 hours Eligibility: Female & Male, 18+, Non-Smoker Possible Side Effects: Soreness, Scarring, Bruising, Temporary Difficulty Walking Schedule A Consultation

TransformAtion After Weight Loss Significant weight loss is a monumental achievement, but it often leaves behind stubborn, sagging skin that diet and exercise cannot address. We specialize in excess skin removal to help you finally see the results of your hard work. Body Lift: This procedure targets the midsection, hips, and lower back by removing the "apron" of skin that can cause discomfort or hygiene issues. It tightens the underlying tissue to create a smoother, firmer body contour. Arm Lift (Brachioplasty): For those dealing with "bat wings" or loose skin on the upper arms, an arm lift removes the excess tissue from the underarm to the elbow, restoring a toned and proportional appearance. By removing excess skin and restoring balance to your contours, these procedures can enhance not only how you look, but how you move, feel, and experience your body every day. FROM CONSULT TO SURGERY DAY Your experience begins with a private consultation where you’ll meet with Dr. Bullocks or Dr. Downey, along with a dedicated patient care coordinator. During this time, we’ll discuss your goals for a body lift or arm lift, review your health history, and perform a thorough evaluation. You’ll be guided through each surgical option in detail, allowing us to create a fully customized treatment plan tailored to your body and desired outcome. You’ll also have the opportunity to ask questions, address any concerns, and review before-and-after photos to feel confident in your decision. Your procedure will take place at our private AAAASF-accredited facility, the gold standard for surgical safety. The Big Day: We will mark the surgical sites and review the final plan with you. While the surgery typically takes several hours depending on the complexity, our specialized anesthesia ensures you remain comfortable and pain-free throughout. Immediate Care: After surgery, you will be monitored by our expert staff in a dedicated recovery room until you are ready to be driven home by a loved one. Follow-Up: We prioritize your healing with a follow-up visit 24 to 72 hours after surgery. You’ll continue with weekly check-ins until your 6-week milestone, at which point most patients are cleared to return to all normal activities. Schedule A Consultation
