At our practice in Houston Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing creates a grid of microscopic treatment zones—often called micro‑channels—within the skin. These controlled columns of thermal energy trigger your body’s natural healing response and stimulate new collagen and elastin. Because the energy is delivered fractionally, untreated skin between the channels speeds recovery while still producing a meaningful change in tone, texture, and firmness. InMode’s platform is FDA‑cleared for the treatment of fine lines, wrinkles, and certain scars, and includes safety features like adjustable depth and real‑time monitoring for consistent outcomes. About InMode Solaria CO₂ Solaria is InMode’s fractional ablative CO₂ platform. It uses radiofrequency‑excited CO₂ microbeams to create a uniform grid of micro‑channels while leaving surrounding skin intact, which helps speed healing and reduces social downtime. Energy and coverage are delivered through an articulated arm and scanner that allow your provider to fine‑tune settings—millijoules, density, scan pattern, and ablation area—for precise, consistent results across the treatment zone. Built‑in safety features support real‑time control and reproducibility, so the treatment can be customized to your skin and goals while maintaining a conservative margin of safety.
