Procedure Overview Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing also known as: laser resurfacing CO2 resurfacing targets photoaging (fine lines, crepiness), dyschromia and sun spots, textural roughness, enlarged pores, acne scars, and selected surgical scars. Addresses Fine lines and wrinkles, Sun damage and uneven skin texture, Acne scars and surgical scars, Enlarged pores and roughness, Irregular skin tone and laxity Procedure Time 30–60 minutes per session, depending on area Eligibility Healthy adults with realistic expectations, Best for lighter to medium skin tones; darker tones require careful evaluation, No active infection, open wounds, or recent isotretinoin use Possible Side Effects What is Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing? At our practice in Houston Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing creates a grid of microscopic treatment zones—often called micro‑channels—within the skin. These controlled columns of thermal energy trigger your body’s natural healing response and stimulate new collagen and elastin. Because the energy is delivered fractionally, untreated skin between the channels speeds recovery while still producing a meaningful change in tone, texture, and firmness. InMode’s platform is FDA‑cleared for the treatment of fine lines, wrinkles, and certain scars, and includes safety features like adjustable depth and real‑time monitoring for consistent outcomes.

About InMode Solaria CO₂ Solaria is InMode’s fractional ablative CO₂ platform. It uses radiofrequency‑excited CO₂ microbeams to create a uniform grid of micro‑channels while leaving surrounding skin intact, which helps speed healing and reduces social downtime. Energy and coverage are delivered through an articulated arm and scanner that allow your provider to fine‑tune settings—millijoules, density, scan pattern, and ablation area—for precise, consistent results across the treatment zone. Built‑in safety features support real‑time control and reproducibility, so the treatment can be customized to your skin and goals while maintaining a conservative margin of safety.

What areas of the body can be treated? Schedule Today Most patients address the face, but treatment can also be appropriate for the neck, chest, and select body areas with photodamage or scarring. Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing Candidates Good CO2 Laser Resurfacing candidates want visible skin rejuvenation without surgery and are comfortable with several days of social downtime. A thorough consultation reviews medical history, prior treatments, skin tone, and any history of abnormal scarring or pigment change. Solaria’s fractional delivery can be tailored for a broad range of complexions; however, patients with deeper skin tones still require careful evaluation and modified protocols to minimize the risk of post‑inflammatory hyperpigmentation. Active infections, uncontrolled medical conditions, recent tanning, or a tendency toward keloids may lead us to recommend alternatives or staged care.

The Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing Process Before, During, and After Laser Resurfacing in Houston 1. Consultation & Planning We assess your skin, review goals, and map a personalized plan. Photographs and baseline measurements help track progress. 2. Comfort & Preparation A topical numbing cream is applied for comfort. Protective eyewear is used. We review aftercare, sunscreen use, and what to expect over the first week. 3. Treatment Your provider passes the handpiece over the treatment area in measured patterns, creating micro‑channels that prompt collagen renewal. Energy, density, and number of passes over the area are customized to your skin and the concern being treated. 4. Immediate Aftercare Skin looks pink to red and feels warm, similar to a sunburn. Cooling, barrier support, and strict sun protection begin the same day. Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing Recovery and Results Redness and swelling are common for up to five days, followed by light peeling or flaking as the skin renews. Makeup is usually appropriate after about one week. Expect visible improvements in texture and tone within one to two weeks, with ongoing remodeling for three to six months as new collagen matures. Many patients see meaningful change after one session; deeper wrinkles or acne scars can benefit from a series of two to three treatments spaced several months apart. Diligent sunscreen, gentle skincare, and any recommended adjunct treatments help maintain results.

Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing Frequently Asked Questions Is Fractional CO2 Laser Resurfacing painful? A topical numbing cream keeps discomfort manageable. Most patients describe a warm, prickling sensation during treatment and temporary tenderness afterward. Can I combine CO2 resurfacing with other treatments? Yes. Many patients pair CO2 with RF microneedling, chemical peels, or injectables to amplify results. Your provider will recommend timing and order. How long until I see results, and how long do they last? Early changes appear in one to two weeks, with continued improvement for three to six months as collagen remodels. Results are long‑lasting with good skincare and sun protection. Will CO2 resurfacing work with my skin type? Solaria’s fractional approach and adjustable settings make CO₂ resurfacing suitable for many skin types. Patients with darker complexions may still face a higher pigment risk, so we individualize energy, density, and aftercare—or recommend alternative treatments—after an in‑person assessment. Is there downtime? Plan for three to five days of redness and swelling, and up to a week before makeup. Full recovery typically completes within seven to ten days with proper aftercare.
