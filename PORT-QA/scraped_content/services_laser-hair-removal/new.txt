See Results What is Laser Hair Removal? Laser hair removal is a treatment that uses concentrated light energy to target hair follicles and reduce future growth. The pigment in the hair absorbs the laser energy, which disrupts the follicle’s ability to keep producing hair the way it did before. It is commonly used on the face and body by patients who want smoother skin and less dependence on shaving, waxing, and tweezing. Because hair grows in cycles, treatment is performed as a series rather than a single appointment.

Procedure Laser hair removal appointments are usually straightforward and efficient. After the skin is prepared, the laser handpiece is passed across the treatment area in a controlled pattern. The energy is delivered directly to the follicles while the surrounding skin is protected. Depending on the size of the area, treatment may take only a few minutes or closer to an hour. Smaller areas like the upper lip move quickly. Larger areas like the legs or back take longer. Most patients describe the sensation as brief heat or a quick snapping feeling. Comfort depends on the treatment area and individual sensitivity, but the procedure is generally well tolerated. Because hair grows in phases, a series of treatments is needed to target follicles at the right point in the cycle. Related Treatments

What to Expect Before, During, and After Laser Hair Removal in Houston 1. Attend a One-on-One Consultation. Prior to getting laser hair removal Houston patients will first meet with a Studio Essex provider for a one-on-one consultation. We take the time to get to know you—your goals, medical history, skin type and tone, hair thickness and density, and the size of your desired treatment area(s). 2. Approve Your Treatment Plan. Your Houston laser hair removal aesthetician will advise you on the ideal options for addressing your unwanted hair. We build a highly personalized treatment plan that will dictate the number of initial sessions you need, as well as a custom long-term maintenance plan. 3. Getting Laser Hair Removal. Your experienced laser technician will glide the laser hair removal device across the treatment area. The light energy targets and destroys the cells responsible for hair growth—with no damage to the surrounding skin. 4. Follow Up. Following laser hair removal Houston patients’ results will not be obvious until about 2 to 3 weeks after treatment. Over the course of your sessions, the speed at which your hair grows will continue to slow; remaining hairs will gradually become less thick and dense. If total hair removal is your goal, you may need between 9 to 12 sessions to completely remove hair due to the body's hair growth cycles. Laser Hair Removal Frequently Asked Questions How many laser hair removal treatments will I need? Most patients need a series of treatments because hair grows in cycles. The exact number depends on the area treated, the density of the hair, and how your body responds. Is laser hair removal permanent? Laser hair removal is considered long-term hair reduction. Many patients experience a major decrease in growth, but some maintenance may still be needed over time. Does laser hair removal hurt? Most patients tolerate treatment well. The sensation is often described as quick heat or a snapping feeling that passes fast. Can laser hair removal treat the face and body? Yes. It is commonly used on areas like the upper lip, chin, underarms, bikini line, chest, back, arms, and legs. Will I need downtime? There is usually little to no downtime. Mild redness or sensitivity may happen, but most patients return to regular activities right away. When will I start seeing results? You may notice changes after the first few treatments, but improvement is gradual. A fuller result develops over the course of the treatment series.
