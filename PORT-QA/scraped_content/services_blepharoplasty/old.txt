Opening Your Eyes to The Possibilities What is Blepharoplasty? Blepharoplasty is a cosmetic procedure designed to improve the appearance of the upper or lower eyelids. Over time, the skin around the eyes begins to stretch, and fat pockets can create puffiness or bags. The blepharoplasty procedure gently corrects these changes by removing excess skin and, when needed, removing or repositioning excess fat. At our practice in Houston blepharoplasty is almost always done on an outpatient basis. Many patients are comfortable with local anesthesia, though sedation or general anesthesia may be an option depending on your needs. Because incisions are placed along the lash line or within the natural crease, they typically heal beautifully and leave little to no visible scarring. What Aesthetic Issues Does Blepharoplasty Address? Eyelid surgery is one of the most versatile facial procedures we perform. Some patients seek it for functional reasons, like heavy upper lids that interfere with vision. Others simply want their eyes to reflect how youthful and energetic they still feel. With a Blepharoplasty Houston patients can improve: Sagging skin on the upper or lower eyelids Under-eye bags or puffiness from shifting fat pockets Loose lower eyelid skin and wrinkles Drooping eyelids that make the eyes look smaller or tired Dark circles that shadows from excess tissue can create Whether you’re considering upper eyelid blepharoplasty, lower blepharoplasty, or a combined quad blepharoplasty, the procedure is tailored to your unique anatomy and goals. Benefits of Blepharoplasty Our patients often tell us eyelid surgery is one of the most rewarding choices they’ve ever made. Just a subtle adjustment around the eyes can make a world of difference. Arrive for Surgery We will provide you with all the ways you can best prepare for the procedure well in advance of your eyelid surgery in Houston. Follow Up Following your blepharoplasty Houston plastic surgeons at Heights Plastic Surgery we will also book you for follow-up visits to meet with your surgeon to monitor the area. This important step ensures you are healing properly, safely, and efficiently. Your Next Best Decision Who is a Candidate? If you’ve been bothered by loose skin, puffy bags, or droopy eyelids, you may be a candidate for blepharoplasty. Many of our patients are in their 30s, 40s, or beyond and are noticing the signs of aging around the eyes for the first time. Others choose this surgical procedure to complement earlier injectable treatments or other facial rejuvenation techniques. Good candidates are in overall good health, don’t smoke, and have realistic expectations for what surgery can achieve. During your initial consultation, we’ll also review your medical history to make sure there aren’t conditions, like certain thyroid problems or chronic dry eyes, that could affect your healing. Get Ready to Shine How to Prepare for Blepharoplasty Preparation is simple but important. At your blepharoplasty consultation, we’ll walk you through every step. You may be asked to pause medications that increase bleeding risk. If you wear contact lenses, plan to use glasses for a short while after surgery. Setting up your recovery space with cold compresses, eye drops, and dark sunglasses will help you feel comfortable at home. Most patients also arrange a friend or family member to drive them home the day of their procedure. See The Difference The Blepharoplasty Procedure Blepharoplasty is always customized, but here’s what you can generally expect: Upper eyelid surgery: We make a small incision in the natural crease of the upper lid, where it will be hidden once healed. Through this incision, we remove excess eyelid skin and sometimes small amounts of fat pockets to open up the eye and restore definition. Lower eyelid surgery: Incisions may be made just beneath the lash line or inside the lid (transconjunctival blepharoplasty). This allows us to remove or reposition fat and smooth the transition between the lower eyelid and cheek. If there’s significant loose skin, it can be gently trimmed and tightened. Quad blepharoplasty: Many patients choose to treat the upper and lower eyelids at the same time for a full rejuvenation. The entire procedure usually takes one to three hours, depending on the extent of correction. Thanks to careful surgical technique and anesthesia, most patients describe the experience as involving only mild discomfort. Rested and Radiating Results You Can Expect Your blepharoplasty results will gradually reveal themselves as swelling fades. The improvements are often dramatic but look completely natural — just a brighter, more rested version of you. Removing excess skin and fat restores a smoother eyelid contour, while repositioning tissues can soften dark circles and under-eye bags. Most patients notice a difference within a couple of weeks, and the outcome continues to refine over the next few months as your body heals. Many say they look years younger, but in the most subtle and believable way. Back at it in No Time Recovery After Eyelid Surgery Recovery from blepharoplasty is usually straightforward. You’ll go home the same day, and we’ll provide detailed aftercare instructions. Keeping your head elevated, using cold compresses, and applying eye drops help reduce swelling and irritation in the first week. It’s also normal to experience some swelling and bruising, blurry vision, or mild tearing at first. Stitches are typically removed within a week, and most people feel comfortable returning to work and normal activities within 7–10 days. Light exercise can usually resume after two weeks. We recommend dark sunglasses outdoors until your eyelids are fully healed to protect your delicate skin. Take a Break Aftercare Tips Following aftercare closely ensures the best results. Use your prescribed eye drops, avoid rubbing your eyes, and hold off on makeup and contact lenses until cleared at your follow-up visit. Some patients combine blepharoplasty with injectable treatments or other facial procedures later to extend their results, but many enjoy the outcome of surgery for a decade or longer. Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About Eyelid Surgery in Houston Can blepharoplasty improve my vision, or is it just cosmetic? Yes, blepharoplasty can improve vision when excess eyelid skin droops into your line of sight. By removing that skin, patients often notice clearer upper vision. For others, it’s mainly a cosmetic procedure to restore a brighter, more youthful look. How long does recovery take, and when will I look “normal” again? Most patients see swelling and bruising fade within 7–10 days. By this point, you can usually return to work and social activities. Final results and incision refinement continue improving over several weeks. Will I have scars after eyelid surgery? Incisions are carefully placed in the natural crease of the upper eyelid or just below the lash line for lower lids. With proper healing, scars fade quickly and become barely visible. Many patients find they are practically undetectable. Am I a good candidate for upper, lower, or both eyelids? If you have drooping upper eyelids, puffy under-eye bags, or loose lower eyelid skin, you may be a candidate. Some benefit from upper eyelid blepharoplasty, some from lower, and others from combining both for the best balance. What are the risks of blepharoplasty? Risks are rare but may include temporary swelling, bruising, dry eyes, or blurry vision. Choosing a board-certified surgeon and following aftercare closely helps minimize complications and ensures safe, natural-looking results.
