Feel free to contact us to learn more about your payment and financing options! What is the difference between cosmetic surgery and plastic surgery? Cosmetic and plastic surgery are typically used synonymously, but do not have the same meaning in the medical world. “Cosmetic surgery” encompasses any procedure whose techniques focus solely on improving the outward appearance of a patient. On the other hand, “plastic surgery” refers to any procedure that involves reconstruction of an area of the body, whether that be scar revision, breast reconstruction, etc. You can learn more about the differences between a cosmetic surgeon and a plastic surgeon from the American Board of Cosmetic Surgery. Drs. Downey and Bullocks at Heights Plastic Surgery offer a wide range of surgical procedures and will educate you on the options that will work best for you during your initial consultation. What is the difference between board certified and non-board certified surgeons? Surgeons can practice medicine with or without board certification. However, board-certified surgeons have taken the extra step to gain recognition from the American Board of Medical Specialties. This certification indicates a dedicated expertise in the surgeon’s field of practice.
