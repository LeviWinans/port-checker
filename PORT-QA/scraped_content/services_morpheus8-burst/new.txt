Morpheus8 Burst—also called collagen induction therapy—is a minimally invasive cosmetic procedure that uses a handheld device with tiny needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin. These micro-injuries stimulate the body’s healing response, triggering increased collagen and elastin production. With Morpheus8 Burst Houston patients can enhance this process by delivering fractional RF energy into deeper layers of the skin in a single pulse. This combination not only smooths fine lines and wrinkles but also targets deeper structural concerns like stretch marks, surgical scars, and skin laxity.

Morpheus8 Burst Frequently Asked Questions Is Microneedling painful? Most patients find the procedure tolerable. A topical numbing cream is applied beforehand to minimize discomfort.
