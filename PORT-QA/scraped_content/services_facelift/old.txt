The facelift is a surgical cosmetic procedure that tightens drooping facial features by strengthening weak facial muscles and removing excess skin and fat. Unlike other temporary solutions like dermal fillers and injectables, the Facelift is a long-lasting solution with results that have the potential to last a lifetime. While the procedure can be performed alone, many patients also undergo eyelid surgery or a brow lift in conjunction with their Facelift for the ultimate rejuvenated and refreshed appearance. Dr. Cara Downey and Dr. Jamal Bullocks are board-certified Houston plastic surgeons with more than 15 years of experience performing Facelifts on men and women in Houston and its surrounding areas with long-lasting and satisfying results. What Aesthetic Issues Does a Facelift Address? While non-surgical treatments can and do improve surface texture, a facelift is capable of handling deeper structural issues. We use facelift surgery to restore youthful contours by targeting specific concerns: Jowl Formation: As facial fat moves downward, it settles along the jawline and blurs your natural contours. Facelift surgery repositions these facial soft tissues to create a crisp profile. Nasolabial Folds: These deep creases from the nose to the mouth appear when facial tissues shift. We lift the superficial musculoaponeurotic system—the SMAS facelift—to soften these lines. Midface Sagging: Flattened cheeks are a hallmark of the aging process. By elevating underlying tissues, we restore the youthful shape of the midface. Loose Neck Skin: Often called a double chin, loose skin and muscle banding below the chin are addressed through a neck lift. Marionette Lines: These vertical folds run from the mouth to the chin. Facelift procedures provide the structural tension needed to smooth this area. Facelift Benefits Getting a Facelift can be a great way to improve your quality of life by addressing various signs of aging and enhancing your appearance. This transformative procedure offers a lot of benefits, including a more youthful appearance, a defined jawline, increased confidence, and long-lasting results. Unlike temporary solutions like fillers and injectables, with a Facelift Houston patients can see long-lasting results that may last a lifetime with proper maintenance. Additionally, it can be combined with other procedures, such as eyelid surgery or brow lift, for comprehensive facial rejuvenation. Structural Correction: A deep plane facelift moves the superficial musculoaponeurotic system to provide a foundation that lasts. Natural Looking Results: Our Surgeons use a surgical technique that avoids a “pulled” look by focusing on vertical lifting. Improved Jawline Definition: By removing excess fat and tightening facial muscles, the surgical procedure restores the angle between the face and neck. Longevity: You will always look younger than if you had no intervention. Comprehensive Rejuvenation: Addressing sagging skin across the face and neck in one surgical procedure reduces total downtime. Am I a candidate for a Facelift? At Heights Plastic Surgery, good candidates for a Facelift experience the following concerns: Moderate to severe fine lines and wrinkles Sagging facial skin Undefined jawline caused by jowls Ideal candidates are in good general health and have realistic expectations of their potential results. The best way to determine if you are a good candidate is to schedule your private consultation with one of our board-certified plastic surgeons. Your Consultation and How to Prepare for a Facelift The process starts in our Houston plastic surgery office with our surgeons. During the facelift consultation, we look at bone structure, skin elasticity, and facial fat to decide between a deep plane facelift surgery, a traditional facelift, or a mini facelift. You will need a thorough pre-operative assessment to establish if you are a safe candidate for the procedure. Avoid blood-thinning agents for two weeks before surgery to help you heal properly with minimal bruising. Because nicotine damages the blood supply, you must be nicotine-free for six weeks surrounding your facial rejuvenation surgery. You also need a responsible adult to stay with you for the first 48 hours. How does a Facelift work? A Facelift is an outpatient procedure that typically takes about three to four hours to complete. To begin, the application of anesthesia will ensure you are comfortable and pain-free throughout your surgery. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will then perform any of the following Facelift techniques. We will discuss the surgical techniques used while developing your personalized treatment plan during your one-on-one consultation. Standard Facelift. The standard procedure aims to alleviate moderate to severe sagging skin on the face and neck. Your surgeon will make incisions along the hairline and behind the ears, giving Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks a better lifting capability. This method results in a longer—but still easily-concealed—scar. Mini-Facelift. This technique is ideal for less severe wrinkled, aged, or sagging facial skin. Incisions are made along the front of the ear and the edge of the hairline to lift the lower face with the jowls. It only addresses neck issues to a small degree but may be combined with neck liposuction to enhance the result. Mid-Facelift. The mid-facelift uses an incision on the outer side of the eyelids to correct signs of aging in the area. This also corrects mild to moderate sagging skin while having only minimal scarring involved. Because a Facelift is typically more about cosmetic appearance and is less of a medical necessity, most insurance plans do not cover it. What is the recovery process after a Facelift? The initial recovery period after a Facelift typically involves mild to moderate swelling and bruising for the first 7 to 10 days. To minimize swelling and promote healing, a compression garment will be provided. Prescription medication may be prescribed by Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks to manage any discomfort you may experience. We also typically recommend for the first two weeks post-surgery that you sleep with your head elevated to help with reducing swelling. Most patients can return to work and resume light daily activities within one to two weeks after surgery. You should avoid direct sunlight exposure and refrain from using heavy makeup for three to four weeks following surgery to protect healing skin. Scars from surgery typically mature into thin, white lines that are easily concealed with makeup within approximately six weeks. At this point, you may gradually resume more strenuous physical activities and exercise routines. While initial improvements will be visible immediately, the full effects will become most apparent once swelling subsides and incisions have completely healed, typically at around three to four months. As with any surgical procedure, there are inherent risks associated with a Facelift. These may include bleeding, infection, scarring, adverse reactions to anesthesia, asymmetry, nerve damage, and changes in sensation. However, by choosing a qualified and experienced plastic surgeon and following pre-operative and post-operative instructions diligently, these risks can be minimized.
