At Heights Plastic Surgery, we offer ezGEL PRF, an advanced treatment that harnesses the body’s natural healing process to rejuvenate skin tone & texture, promote collagen production and subtly restore lost volume. This minimally invasive procedure uses platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) derived from the patient’s blood to stimulate tissue regeneration, creating long-lasting and natural results. Our expert medical spa team in Houston, TX, provides customized treatment plans to address a range of aesthetic concerns, ensuring optimal results with a safe and effective procedure. What is ezGEL? ezGEL is a 100% natural bio-stimulating filler created from a patient’s platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) and albumin, offering a revolutionary alternative to synthetic dermal fillers. This gel-like texture treatment provides subtle volumizing effects while stimulating collagen production, leading to gradual, natural beauty enhancements. It is commonly used for facial rejuvenation, hair restoration, and skin health improvements without the risk of allergic reactions or synthetic substances. ezGEL also known as: PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)

Frequently Asked Questions Jump to FAQs How is Platelet-Rich Fibrin Harvested? ezGEL PRF is created from a small blood draw processed using a high-speed centrifuge to isolate platelet-rich fibrin, white blood cells, mesenchymal stem cells, and growth factors. The separated components are then combined into a gel form, which is ready for injection into the treatment area.
