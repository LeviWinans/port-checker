Procedure Overview ezGEL also known as: PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin) Addresses Fine lines, wrinkles, and sagging skin, Dark circles and hollows under the eyes, Subtle volume loss in the face, neck, and hands, Hair loss and promoting hair growth, Overall skin rejuvenation and improved skin texture Procedure Time 45-60 minutes Eligibility Individuals experiencing skin laxity, fine lines & wrinkles, Patients seeking to improve dark circles and crepiness under the eyes, Individuals experiencing mild volume loss, Patients seeking a natural filler alternative to synthetic dermal fillers, Those looking for hair restoration or tissue regeneration, Good candidates include those in overall good health without blood disorders Possible Side Effects Patients may experience mild swelling, bruising or redness. What is ezGEL? ezGEL is a 100% natural bio-stimulating filler created from a patient’s platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) and albumin, offering a revolutionary alternative to synthetic dermal fillers. This gel-like texture treatment provides subtle volumizing effects while stimulating collagen production, leading to gradual, natural beauty enhancements. It is commonly used for facial rejuvenation, hair restoration, and skin health improvements without the risk of allergic reactions or synthetic substances.

How is Platelet-Rich Fibrin Harvested? ezGEL PRF is created from a small blood draw processed using a high-speed centrifuge to isolate platelet-rich fibrin, white blood cells, mesenchymal stem cells, and growth factors. The separated components are then combined into a gel form, which is ready for injection into the treatment area. This safe and effective procedure maximizes tissue regeneration while minimizing discomfort.
