Benefits of an IPL Photofacial The primary advantage of intense pulsed light over traditional ablative lasers is the preservation of the skin’s surface. You receive deep cellular correction while keeping the epidermal barrier intact. No Surface Damage: The light passes straight through the top layer of skin. Broad Coverage: We can safely treat the face, neck, chest, and hands in a single session. Quick Treatment: A full facial pass takes less than 30 minutes. Collagen Stimulation: The thermal heat prompts a secondary healing response, mildly firming the skin texture. Candidates for an IPL Photofacial Safety and efficacy rely heavily on your natural skin tone. We assess your Fitzpatrick skin type during your consultation. Fair to Medium Skin Tones: IPL targets contrast. Dark spots on lighter skin absorb the light perfectly. Darker skin tones risk absorbing too much heat, which can lead to blistering or worsened hyperpigmentation. Sun-Protected Skin: You cannot have an active tan or a sunburn on the day of treatment. Non-Pregnant Patients: Hormonal shifts during pregnancy make pigmentation unpredictable. How to Prepare for an IPL Photofacial Your pre-treatment habits directly dictate how your skin reacts to the light pulses. Two Weeks Prior: Stop using topical retinols, Retin-A, and any exfoliating acids. Discontinue taking antibiotics, as they drastically increase photosensitivity. Sun Avoidance: Stay out of direct sunlight. Wear a physical SPF 50 every single day. Day of Treatment: Arrive at Studio Essex with a completely clean face. Remove all makeup, lotions, and sunscreens before sitting in the chair. The Procedure Your Houston IPL Photofacial begins with your provider cleansing the target area to remove any residual oils. We apply a clear, cooling gel to the skin to enhance light penetration and protect the epidermis. You are given dark protective eyewear. We calibrate the device settings to match your specific target concerns—adjusting the wavelength to hit either red blood vessels or brown melanin. The handpiece is passed sequentially across your skin. The device emits bright flashes of light. Most patients describe the physical sensation as a quick, hot rubber band snapping against the skin. We wipe away the gel and apply a calming barrier cream.

Results: What to Expect The correction happens in specific physical stages. Immediate Post-Op: Your skin will look slightly pink. The targeted brown spots will immediately look much darker. Days 3–5: The darkened spots rise to the surface of the skin. They resemble tiny coffee grounds. Days 7–10: These dark flakes naturally slough off. You will see clearer, more even skin underneath. Final Outcome: We recommend a series of three to six treatments spaced a few weeks apart to completely clear deep pigmentation. Recovery Timeline There is no formal physical downtime required. You can drive yourself back to work immediately after leaving our Houston plastic surgery clinic. First 24 Hours: Expect mild swelling and a sensation similar to a light sunburn. You can apply cool compresses to the area. Day 2: You can resume wearing makeup to cover the darkening spots. One Week: Your normal skincare routine can resume once all flaking has stopped. Aftercare for Optimal Healing Your skin will be highly vulnerable to UV rays while it sheds the pigmented cells. Strict Sun Protection: You must wear a physical, zinc-based sunscreen daily. Wear a wide-brimmed hat if you step outside. No Picking: Do not scrub or pick at the dark spots as they rise to the surface. Let them fall off naturally to prevent scarring. Avoid Heat: Skip the sauna, hot yoga, and hot showers for the first 48 hours. Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About IPL Photofacial in Houston What is an IPL photofacial? The IPL Photofacial is a unique facial rejuvenating treatment that utilizes the power of light energy to correct hyper-pigmented skin around your treated areas, typically the face, neck, and décolletage. Many laser treatments damage the surface of the skin and require a long recovery period. The IPL Photofacial, however, uses different wavelengths of intense pulsed light to penetrate the deeper layers of your skin and target hyper-pigmentation. All without damaging your skin. The pigmented cells simply absorb the heat from the light energy, die off, and are naturally eliminated from the body, leaving you with an even complexion. How much does an IPL photofacial cost in Houston? The average cost of an IPL Photofacial in Houston typically starts at $350. At Studio Essex, we accept cash and all major credit cards. We are also happy to offer financing through CareCredit® for patients. How does an IPL photofacial work? If you are planning on getting an IPL treatment in Houston, it is imperative that you avoid prolonged sun exposure, topical retinol/retinoic acid, and antibiotics for 2 weeks prior to treatment. This is a precaution that helps prevent sun burns and complications from treatment. Plus, you cannot receive treatment if you have a sunburn! Before your IPL Photofacial treatment begins, Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will prepare your skin by thoroughly cleaning the desired treated area. This will ensure you will remain as comfortable as possible throughout the entirety of your treatment. Once numb, Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will lay you down on one of our comfortable procedure tables and provide you with protective eyewear. They will then start applying bursts of intense pulsed light onto your desired treated area. While many patients are fairly comfortable throughout this procedure, most describe the feeling as being snapped with a rubber band. Each treatment session typically takes about 20 to 30 minutes to complete. What can I expect after my IPL photofacial? Because the photofacial is a minimally invasive treatment, there is no formal downtime or recovery period associated with the procedure. Most patients can return to their normal daily activities, including work, following their IPL Photofacial treatment. Following treatment, many patients experience their skin being red, swollen, and sensitive to the sun for the next few days. This is normal and typically resolves on its own. Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks advise moisturizing your face and applying medical-grade sunscreen daily to ensure a smooth and swift recovery. While many patients may start to see the results of their IPL Photofacial after just one treatment, Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks typically recommend a series of three to six treatments spaced a few weeks apart to achieve ultimate results. Am I a good candidate for an IPL photofacial? Not everyone is a candidate for IPL, which is why it is so important to have an initial consultation beforehand with a trained medical provider to ensure it is the best form of treatment for your skin concerns. Good candidates for the IPL photofacial are in good general health and have realistic expectations of the results they can expect from their treatment.
