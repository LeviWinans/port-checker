A Mommy Makeover is a blanket term used for a group of procedures that women may choose to undergo to restore their bodies after having children. As a mother of twins, Dr. Cara Downey has focused a large part of her practice on restoring the female figure after childbirth via breast enhancement surgeries, tummy tucks, and other rejuvenation procedures. Collectively, these procedures reverse any undesirable physical changes that may have occurred after childbirth. While these surgeries can be performed separately, effectively combining them into a single procedure takes a highly talented and experienced board-certified plastic surgeon. Dr. Cara Downey and Dr. Jamal Bullocks have delivered beautiful and consistent Mommy Makeover results to hundreds of women in Texas. What procedures are included in a Mommy Makeover? A Mommy Makeover is a personalized combination of any of the following cosmetic procedures: Tummy Tuck. A tummy tuck is the primary element in a Mommy Makeover. This procedure covers everything from the repair of separated abdominal muscles, the ridding of excess skin and fat, and the removal of stretch marks. Additionally, a tummy tuck can alter the position of the belly button on the mid-section. Liposuction. Liposuction removes excess fat from the woman’s stomach, back, arms, or thighs. This procedure is often done in conjunction with a tummy tuck to help sculpt and shape the silhouette. Breast Enhancement. The breast enhancement portion of the Mommy Makeover surgery usually combines a breast lift with breast augmentation or a breast reduction. When combined, these procedures can alter the shape, position, and size of the breasts. Brazilian Butt Lift. A Brazilian butt lift involves the use of harvested fat from the patient’s body to lift and sculpt your buttocks. Renuvion. Skin tightening also enhances the results of liposuction. We use a special plasma technology called Renuvion to achieve skin tightening for our Houston Mommy Makeover patients. Non-surgical procedures. Patients may also choose to undergo non-surgical cosmetic procedures as part of their Mommy Makeovers, such as dermal fillers. Mommy Makeover Benefits A Mommy Makeover can significantly enhance your quality of life by addressing the physical changes that occur after pregnancy and childbirth. Youthful Appearance: Non-surgical procedures like dermal fillers can further enhance facial features and reduce signs of aging. Am I a good candidate for a Mommy Makeover? At Heights Plastic Surgery, individuals well-suited for a Mommy Makeover typically meet the following requirements: Have noticed undesired physical changes post-childbirth. Have completed breastfeeding at least six months ago. Are 18 years of age or older. Do not plan on having more children. Maintain a Body Mass Index (BMI) within the recommended range. Enjoy good overall health and are non-smokers. Hold realistic expectations about achieving their desired physique. After Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks confirm that you are a candidate for a Mommy Makeover, they will discuss your personalized treatment plan with you. Learn More Frequently Asked Questions About Mommy Makeover What can I expect in recovery after a Mommy Makeover? The recovery period of a Mommy Makeover can be very challenging. It is important to schedule your regular follow-up visits to Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks’ office to ensure you are healing with no complications. For the first 24 hours, you may experience some pain and discomfort as your anesthesia wears off. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will provide you with prescription medication to help you manage this discomfort before your discharge. You may have surgical drains to help control fluid build-up in your body and be asked to wear surgical compression garments to promote healing. Within about two to three weeks following your Mommy Makeover, your surgical drains may be removed, and you may return to work and low-impact daily activities. Most patients report getting used to their new bodies after about one month, when most swelling and redness subsides. Swelling, bruising, and the use of your surgical bra will typically last about six weeks. We may ask you to continue using your other compression garments for up to 90 days following your procedure to ensure the ultimate results. What are the risks associated with a Mommy Makeover? While Mommy Makeover procedures are generally safe, like any surgery, they come with inherent risks. Potential risks include infection, bleeding, adverse reactions to anesthesia, scarring, and asymmetry. However, by choosing a board-certified plastic surgeon and following post-operative instructions diligently, these risks can be minimized. Can I get a Mommy Makeover while pregnant or breastfeeding? No, it is not recommended to undergo a Mommy Makeover while pregnant or breastfeeding. Pregnancy and breastfeeding can significantly impact the body, and undergoing cosmetic surgery during these periods can pose risks to both the mother and the baby. It’s best to wait until you have completed breastfeeding and your body has stabilized before considering a Mommy Makeover. Can I get a Mommy Makeover if I plan on having more children? While it’s technically possible to undergo a Mommy Makeover if you plan on having more children, it’s generally advised to wait until you have completed your family. Pregnancy and childbirth can undo the results of cosmetic surgery, necessitating additional procedures. By waiting until you’re done having children, you can ensure longer-lasting results and avoid the need for repeat surgeries. How much does a mommy makeover cost in Houston?
