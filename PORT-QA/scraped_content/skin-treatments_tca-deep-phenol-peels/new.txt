Skin Resurfacing Chemical Peels Ready for a radical result? Our Chemical Peel treatments give surgical results, without going under the knife. The TCA Peel and the Deep Phenol Peel are skin resurfacing treatments that effectively remove the outer layers of sun-damaged, blemished, unevenly pigmented, blotchy skin and pre-cancerous growths. These more intensive peels require a preliminary consultation with Dr. Villano where you will discuss your goals and expectations and together develop a personalized treatment plan appropriate to your needs. Additional Reading These peels typically provide the most dramatic results in wrinkle reduction with vigorous post-treatment collagen production that can take as much as 10 years off the face. A chemical peel is frequently performed in conjunction with a facelift although it is not considered a substitute for a facelift, since it addresses skin surface issues. Preparation for Chemical Peel Treatments As with any procedure, Dr. Villano will discuss your concerns and help you set a personalized treatment plan to achieve your goals. You should be in good health and be able to establish realistic goals and expectations. A nutritious diet is beneficial and we prefer you stop smoking for a few weeks before and after any invasive procedure. All peel procedures carry some risks, and these should be discussed with Dr. Villano during your consultation.
