Recovery and Post-Treatment Care Schedule Today Minimal Downtime: Most dermatology services, like chemical peels and laser treatments, require minimal recovery, with some redness or mild irritation that subsides within a few days. Aftercare: For treatments like microneedling or chemical peels, it’s important to avoid sun exposure and follow the recommended skincare regimen to support healing and enhance results. Results When to Expect Results: Many patients see improvements in skin clarity and texture within 2-4 weeks, with continued enhancements as treatments progress. Longevity of Results: Results vary depending on the procedure. Regular skincare maintenance and follow-up treatments can prolong the benefits, with some results lasting several months or longer.

Dermatology Services Frequently Asked Questions How often should I schedule a skin cancer screening? It’s recommended to have a skin cancer screening once a year, but more frequent checks may be needed if you have a history of skin cancer or concerning lesions. What prescription options are available for treating my skin condition? We offer a range of prescription treatments tailored to your needs, including topical creams, oral medications, and more advanced solutions for acne, rosacea, hyperpigmentation, and aging. How soon can I expect results from acne or hyperpigmentation treatments? Results typically start to show within 2-4 weeks, but long-term improvements often require consistent care and follow-up treatments. Can I combine prescription treatments with in-office procedures? Yes, combining prescription medications with in-office procedures can enhance and speed up your results, offering a more comprehensive approach to skin care. How much do Dermatology Services cost? The cost varies based on the specific treatment plan. We’ll provide a detailed estimate during your consultation based on your individual needs.
