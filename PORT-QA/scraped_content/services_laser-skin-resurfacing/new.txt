See Results What Is Laser Skin Resurfacing? Laser skin resurfacing is a nonsurgical treatment that uses controlled light energy to improve skin tone, texture, pigmentation, and visible signs of aging. Fractional technology treats microscopic sections of skin while leaving surrounding tissue intact, supporting faster healing and progressive collagen renewal. At Studio Essex at Heights Plastic Surgery, Fraxel® laser skin resurfacing offers a precise way to refresh sun-damaged, scarred, or uneven skin. Treatment settings are selected according to your skin tone, concerns, treatment area, and tolerance for downtime. Some patients want a subtle polish. Others are ready for more corrective resurfacing. The treatment plan should reflect that difference. Continue Fraxel can be used on the face and select areas of the body, including the neck, chest, and hands. These high-exposure areas often reveal accumulated sun damage and textural changes before the rest of the skin does.

What Aesthetic Issues Does Laser Skin Resurfacing Address? Laser skin resurfacing treats concerns that develop within the skin itself. How to Prepare for Laser Skin Resurfacing Thoughtful preparation helps reduce irritation, pigment changes, and unnecessary delays in healing. Limit sun exposure. Avoid tanning and protect the treatment area with broad-spectrum sunscreen before your appointment. Review your medications. Tell your provider about prescriptions, supplements, recent antibiotics, and products that increase light sensitivity. Pause irritating skincare when instructed. Retinoids, exfoliating acids, scrubs, and other active products may need to be stopped temporarily. Avoid competing treatments. Waxing, chemical peels, aggressive facials, and other procedures should not be performed too close to your laser appointment. Discuss your history. Let your provider know about cold sores, pigment changes, scarring, previous laser treatments, and difficulty healing. Plan around your calendar. Although Fraxel involves less recovery than deeper ablative resurfacing, visible redness, swelling, bronzing, or flaking may affect social plans for several days. Arrive with clean skin. Unless directed otherwise, avoid makeup, self-tanner, and heavy skincare products on the treatment area.

What Is the Laser Skin Resurfacing Procedure Like? Your appointment begins with a review of your skin, treatment goals, and the areas being addressed. The skin is thoroughly cleansed, and protective eyewear is used throughout the laser portion of the visit. A topical numbing cream is typically applied before treatment. Once the skin is ready, your provider moves the Fraxel handpiece across the selected area in a controlled pattern. The device creates microscopic treatment zones at the chosen depth while leaving surrounding skin intact. Most patients describe the sensation as increasing warmth, prickling, or quick elastic-like snaps. Integrated cooling helps manage heat during treatment. The “ouch factor” is generally moderate and brief, though comfort varies by treatment intensity and individual sensitivity. The full visit may take approximately 50 to 70 minutes, including preparation and numbing. Treating multiple areas may require additional time. Before you leave, your provider will review skincare, sun protection, and the expected stages of recovery.

Results from Laser Skin Resurfacing Fraxel results develop in stages as surface renewal and collagen remodeling progress. Some patients notice a meaningful change after one session. A series of three to five treatments, often spaced several weeks apart, may be recommended for more significant sun damage, scarring, or texture concerns. Results vary with treatment intensity, skin condition, and the body’s healing response. The First Few Days The skin may initially look pink, swollen, or slightly bronzed. It can feel warm and tight, similar to a sunburn. Flaking or a rough, sandpaper-like texture may develop as damaged cells move toward the surface. After One to Two Weeks Once the initial recovery phase settles, many patients notice brighter color, smoother texture, and a fresher overall appearance. Pigmented spots may temporarily darken before fading or shedding. Over the Following Months Collagen remodeling continues beneath the surface. Fine lines, scars, and textural irregularities may soften gradually, with more complete improvement often visible two to three months after treatment. Recovery After Laser Skin Resurfacing Fraxel generally involves less recovery than fully ablative laser resurfacing, but “minimal downtime” does not always mean invisible downtime. Immediate Downtime Redness, warmth, mild swelling, and tenderness are common immediately after treatment. These effects may feel similar to a sunburn and usually begin easing within several hours. Social Downtime Some patients feel comfortable returning to work or routine activities quickly. More corrective sessions may produce several days of redness, bronzing, dryness, or flaking. Patients preparing for photographs, meetings, or special events should leave enough room for the skin to settle. Exercise, Makeup, Sun, and Skincare Your provider may recommend temporarily avoiding: Strenuous exercise and excessive sweating Hot showers, saunas, and steam rooms Makeup until the skin has calmed Retinoids, acids, scrubs, and exfoliating products Direct sun exposure Swimming pools or other potential sources of irritation Recovery following laser skin resurfacing Varies according to the treatment area, settings used, and your skin’s sensitivity. Following the individualized plan matters more than following a generic timeline. Aftercare Freshly resurfaced skin needs protection and restraint. A simple routine usually serves it best during the early healing phase. Houston sun exposure makes diligent protection especially important. Consistent sunscreen use helps protect healing skin, limit new pigmentation, and preserve the improvement created by treatment. After treatment: Cleanse the skin gently with provider-approved products Apply moisturizer as directed Wear broad-spectrum sunscreen with an SPF of 30 or higher Continue Avoid direct sun and wear a hat outdoors Do not scrub, pick, or peel flaking skin Pause active skincare until your provider clears it Use cool compresses if recommended Attend follow-up visits as scheduled Contact the practice if redness, swelling, pain, or blistering worsens unexpectedly

Laser Skin Resurfacing Frequently Asked Questions What is laser skin resurfacing? Laser skin resurfacing is a minimally invasive, non-surgical cosmetic treatment that evens out your unwanted hyperpigmented skin and uneven skin texture for the ultimate smooth complexion. At Studio Essex, we use the Fraxel® laser to improve your overall skin tone, reduce your sun damage, and improve the appearance of your acne scarring. The Fraxel® laser technology is designed with cooling technology to give you smoother and younger-looking skin with minimal discomfort and minimal downtime. How much does laser skin resurfacing cost in Houston? The average cost of laser skin resurfacing with the Fraxel® laser in Houston typically starts at $2500. The total cost for your laser skin resurfacing treatment will depend on factors such as your surgeon’s fee, the location of your procedure, and other miscellaneous medical expenses. At Studio Essex, we accept cash and all major credit cards. We are also happy to offer financing through CareCredit® for patients. What does a resurfacing session look like? Before your laser skin resurfacing treatment Houston aesthetic experts Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will prepare your skin by thoroughly cleaning and applying a topical anesthetic to your desired treated area. This will ensure you will remain as comfortable as possible throughout the entirety of your treatment. Once numb, Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will lay you down on one of our comfortable procedure tables and provide you with protective eyewear. She will then start applying bursts of the Fraxel® laser onto your desired treated area. While many patients are fairly comfortable throughout this procedure, most describe the feeling as though their skin is being snapped with a rubber band. Each treatment session typically takes under an hour to complete. What can I expect after a laser skin resurfacing treatment? Because laser skin resurfacing with the Fraxel® laser is a minimally invasive treatment, there is no formal downtime or recovery period associated with the procedure. Most Houston laser skin resurfacing patients are able to return to their normal daily activities, including work, following their laser skin resurfacing treatment. Following treatment with the Fraxel® laser, many patients experience their skin being red, swollen, and sensitive to the sun for the next few hours. This is normal and typically resolves on its own. Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks advises you to moisturize your face and apply a medical-grade sunscreen every day to ensure a smooth and swift recovery. While many patients may start to see the results of their laser skin resurfacing treatment after just one session, Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks typically recommends a series of three to five treatments spaced two to four weeks apart to achieve ultimate results.
