An augmentation mastopexy is a highly integrated cosmetic surgical procedure. Rather than choosing either a breast augmentation or a lift, this surgical procedure combines both. It utilizes your choice of implant type to restore lost upper-pole fullness, while the surgeon simultaneously elevates the nipple-areolar complex to a higher, tighter position on the chest wall by removing excess skin. We do not treat this as two separate operations pieced together. The size and profile of the implants dictate the exact surgical techniques required to create a perfectly tensioned, youthful appearance with natural-looking results. What concerns does combining these procedures treat? For our patients in Houston breast augmentation with lift directly resolves mechanical and structural failures within the breast tissue. We utilize this dual-approach cosmetic surgery to correct: Severe Breast Sagging (Ptosis): When the nipple falls below the level of the breast fold (the inframammary fold or breast crease). Nipple-Areolar Descent: Nipples that point downward toward the floor rather than forward, often accompanied by stretched, enlarged areolas. Post-Pregnancy Deflation: The “empty” appearance of the breasts following breastfeeding, where glandular tissue shrinks rapidly, leaving excess skin and excess breast tissue behind. Loss of Breast Volume: A hollow, flat appearance across the top of the chest that diminishes overall breast aesthetics. Body Contouring Balance: For patients with wider hips or broad shoulders, adding volume to the chest creates a more balanced, hourglass silhouette. What are the benefits of Breast Augmentation with Lift? The primary benefit of a breast augmentation with lift is achieving complete structural harmony in a single operating room session. Complete Proportion: You gain the exact cup size you desire while simultaneously ensuring the breasts sit high and tight on the chest wall. Enhanced Cleavage: The implants provide medial fullness, while the lift ensures the breast tissue supports the implant tightly against the sternum. Areolar Refinement: We permanently reduce the circumference of stretched areolas during the lifting phase, restoring a highly aesthetic focal point. Who are the ideal candidates for this surgery? Surgical intervention requires appropriate patient selection. Prior to a breast augmentation with lift Houston plastic surgeons at our surgery center will evaluate the elasticity of your skin and your specific cosmetic goals to determine if you require both procedures. You may be a good candidate if… Women experiencing “deflated” breasts post-pregnancy or significant weight loss are typically excellent candidates. If you pull your skin upward in the mirror and realize you need that tightness, but you also desire the roundness of an implant, this procedure is the correct choice. You must be at a stable body weight, as drastic weight fluctuations can restretch the tissue. This may not be the right fit if… If you only require a volume reduction to alleviate back pain, a standalone breast reduction is appropriate. If you are currently pregnant or plan to have more children in the near future, we highly recommend waiting, as milk production will undo the lift. Furthermore, if you are an active smoker, you are entirely disqualified until you complete six weeks of strict nicotine abstinence. Related Procedures

The breast augmentation with lift is performed in our private, fully accredited surgical facility. You are placed under general anesthesia by a board-certified anesthesiologist, ensuring absolute comfort. The Incision Pattern: Depending on the severity of your sagging, your surgeon utilizes a specific pattern (peri-areolar, vertical, or anchor) to allow for the precise removal of excess skin. Implant Placement: We create a precise pocket, typically submuscular, to support the weight of the implant and provide a smooth, natural upper slope. Tissue Reshaping: We draw the surrounding breast tissue tightly over the implant and excise all redundant skin from the lower pole of the breast. Deep Closure: We close the incisions using multiple layers of deep, dissolving sutures. This internal scaffolding supports the weight of the reconstruction and minimizes tension on the external skin lines. Can this be combined with other procedures? Absolutely. Many patients seeking comprehensive body contour changes integrate additional procedures to utilize a single recovery window. Social Downtime: Expect to take 7 to 10 days away from the office. You will return to our clinic for routine follow-up visits to ensure the incisions are healing flawlessly. Physical Downtime: You must strictly avoid elevating your heart rate or lifting heavy objects for a minimum of four to six weeks. Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions about Breast Augmentation with Lift Can I still breastfeed after this surgery? While many women retain the ability to breastfeed, the surgery does require repositioning the nipple and rearranging glandular tissue, which can potentially sever milk ducts. If future breastfeeding is a top priority for you, we generally recommend waiting until your family is complete to undergo a lift. Will my breasts sag again in the future? The surgery permanently resets the clock, but it does not stop the aging process. Over a decade or more, gravity, natural skin elasticity loss, and hormonal changes will slowly affect the breast tissue. However, your breasts will always remain higher and more youthful than they would have been without the surgery. Do I need to replace the implants eventually? Breast implants are medical devices and are not considered lifetime devices. While modern cohesive gel (gummy bear) implants are incredibly durable, most patients should anticipate replacing or exchanging their implants every 10 to 15 years due to natural wear, capsular contracture, or changing aesthetic preferences.
