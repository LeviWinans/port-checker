An Elevated, Proportionate Profile. Am i a Candidate? Architectural restoration. Rebuilt volume. An elevated, proportionate profile. When it comes to breasts, achieving a balanced look is important. If there’s fullness without any lift, it can create a heavy appearance at the bottom. On the other hand, if there’s a lift without enough volume, the breasts can appear flat. To maintain a youthful and proportionate look, both fullness and support are necessary. After experiences like pregnancy, significant weight loss, or just the natural aging process, breast tissue often changes. It can lose volume, and the ligaments that support the breasts may stretch, leading to sagging skin. Simply adding breast implants under stretched skin won’t solve the problem; it can actually result in larger yet still sagging breasts. At Heights Plastic Surgery, we see breast enhancement as a complex task that requires careful planning. For patients in Houston looking for breast augmentation with lift, we offer a solution that addresses both issues at once. By combining implants with a lift procedure, we can restore lost volume and tighten the skin, resulting in a youthful, firm look that can boost your confidence and enhance your natural beauty. What is an Augmentation Mastopexy? An augmentation mastopexy is a highly integrated cosmetic surgical procedure. Rather than choosing either a breast augmentation or a lift, this surgical procedure combines both. It utilizes your choice of implant type to restore lost upper-pole fullness, while the surgeon simultaneously elevates the nipple-areolar complex to a higher, tighter position on the chest wall by removing excess skin. We do not treat this as two separate operations pieced together. The size and profile of the implants dictate the exact surgical techniques required to create a perfectly tensioned, youthful appearance with natural-looking results.

What concerns does combining these procedures treat? For our patients in Houston breast augmentation with lift directly resolves mechanical and structural failures within the breast tissue. We utilize this dual-approach cosmetic surgery to correct: Severe Breast Sagging (Ptosis): When the nipple falls below the level of the breast fold (the inframammary fold or breast crease). Nipple-Areolar Descent: Nipples that point downward toward the floor rather than forward, often accompanied by stretched, enlarged areolas. Post-Pregnancy Deflation: The “empty” appearance of the breasts following breastfeeding, where glandular tissue shrinks rapidly, leaving excess skin and excess breast tissue behind. Loss of Breast Volume: A hollow, flat appearance across the top of the chest that diminishes overall breast aesthetics. Body Contouring Balance: For patients with wider hips or broad shoulders, adding volume to the chest creates a more balanced, hourglass silhouette.

The breast augmentation with lift is performed in our private, fully accredited surgical facility. You are placed under general anesthesia by a board-certified anesthesiologist, ensuring absolute comfort. The Incision Pattern: Depending on the severity of your sagging, your surgeon utilizes a specific pattern (peri-areolar, vertical, or anchor) to allow for the precise removal of excess skin. Tissue Reshaping: We draw the surrounding breast tissue tightly over the implant and excise all redundant skin from the lower pole of the breast. Deep Closure: We close the incisions using multiple layers of deep, dissolving sutures. This internal scaffolding supports the weight of the reconstruction and minimizes tension on the external skin lines.

Breast Augmentation with Lift Frequently Asked Questions Can I still breastfeed after this surgery? While many women retain the ability to breastfeed, the surgery does require repositioning the nipple and rearranging glandular tissue, which can potentially sever milk ducts. If future breastfeeding is a top priority for you, we generally recommend waiting until your family is complete to undergo a lift. Will my breasts sag again in the future? The surgery permanently resets the clock, but it does not stop the aging process. Over a decade or more, gravity, natural skin elasticity loss, and hormonal changes will slowly affect the breast tissue. However, your breasts will always remain higher and more youthful than they would have been without the surgery. Do I need to replace the implants eventually? Breast implants are medical devices and are not considered lifetime devices. While modern cohesive gel (gummy bear) implants are incredibly durable, most patients should anticipate replacing or exchanging their implants every 10 to 15 years due to natural wear, capsular contracture, or changing aesthetic preferences.
