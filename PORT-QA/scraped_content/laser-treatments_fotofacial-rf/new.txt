An improved complexion is only a snap away with the ELOS Plus photorejuvenation treatment. Fotofacial RF skin rejuvenation treats vascular lesions, pigmented skin lesions (age or sun spots), rosacea and other skin texture irregularities without damaging skin tissue. ELOS Plus merges electrical and optical energy in controlled pulses to safely and effectively treat unwanted skin imperfections. Because ELOS Plus is non-ablative, there is none of the downtime associated with chemical peels and laser skin resurfacing used in the treatment of deep wrinkles, precancerous lesions, deep acne scars and other skin damage.
