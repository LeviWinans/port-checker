Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About Butt Implants in Houston What are butt implants? Buttock implants are specialized silicone implants used to improve the overall shape, size, and feel of the buttocks. Butt implants are nearly always made from silicone gel, unlike breast implants that are also available in saline and silicone variants. The silicone gel inside our buttock implants provides a soft, yet firm form that allows them to keep their original dimensions even after insertion. What should I expect in recovery after a butt implant surgery? For the first 24 hours following your Houston butt implant surgery, it is normal to experience some pain, soreness, swelling, and bruising around your treated areas. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will provide you with prescription medication that can help manage your pain. It is important that you refrain from sitting on your bottom or laying on your back for 2 to 3 weeks following your butt implant surgery. Doing so may compromise your expected results. While many patients are able to see some visible results immediately following surgery, ultimate results will become apparent after all swelling and bruising has faded, about three to six months later.
