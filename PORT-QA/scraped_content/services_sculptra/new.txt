What is Sculptra? Sculptra is an injectable treatment composed of poly-L-lactic acid, a biocompatible synthetic material that works deep in the skin. While many traditional dermal fillers use hyaluronic acid to add volume, Sculptra injections trigger your body’s natural collagen production. It’s a great option for most patients in Houston who want gradual improvement that settles over time. This aesthetic treatment provides structural support and improved skin quality rather than an immediate and temporary artificial plump.

What Aesthetic Issues Does Sculptra Address? Sculptra works to reinforce the skin’s structural foundation by addressing the biological signs of aging. Candidates for Sculptra Schedule Today Ideal Sculptra candidates are anyone concerned about volume loss or deep facial lines who want to achieve optimal results through a gradual process. Sculptra is suited for patients who have the patience for a result that builds over multiple sessions. Safety protocols at Heights Plastic Surgery and Studio Essex Medical Spa require us to screen your medical history for specific health factors. You should skip this treatment if you have a history of keloid formation or hypertrophic scarring. Our team reviews your medical history during the initial consultation to ensure your aesthetic goals can be met safely. Consultation and Preparation for Sculptra Your Houston Sculptra consultation includes a physical assessment of your bone structure and skin elasticity. Our clinical team of specialists determines if your anatomy will respond best to Sculptra, or if other non-surgical products and services, or even a plastic surgery procedure option would be a better option. Preparation is straightforward. Avoid taking blood thinners or supplements for one week to minimize the risk of mild swelling or bruising at the injection sites. We also recommend disclosing all medications to avoid potential risks during the healing process.

The Sculptra Procedure The Sculptra treatment takes place at Heights Plastic Surgery and Studio Essex Medical Spa and usually lasts between 30 and 45 minutes. We apply a topical numbing cream or topical anesthetic to ensure you feel generally mild discomfort during the cosmetic procedures. Your provider uses a fine needle to place the formula into the underlying tissues in the treatment area.

Sculptra Recovery Sculptra requires no formal downtime, you can return to your daily life right away. You may notice mild swelling or redness at the injection sites, but this typically fades within a few weeks. We recommend that patients follow the 5-5-5 rule: massage the treatment area for five minutes, five times a day, for five days to ensure the treatment is evenly distributed.

Sculptra Results Schedule Today Changes are not visible right away; optimal Sculptra results take time as the body works to produce collagen. Real results emerge as Sculptra stimulates your natural collagen production over a few months. Most patients see their desired results about three to six months after their final sessions. To achieve optimal results, we usually recommend a personalized treatment plan of three sessions. Sculptra Aftercare Rigorous sun protection is vital to extend your youthful skin, as UV rays break down the natural collagen you are building. Maintaining a healthy lifestyle supports the biological process required for collagen production. Regular touch-up visits every year or two help maintain long-lasting facial rejuvenation.
