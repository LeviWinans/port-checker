Sculptra is an injectable treatment composed of poly-L-lactic acid, a biocompatible synthetic material that works deep in the skin. While many traditional dermal fillers use hyaluronic acid to add volume, Sculptra injections trigger your body’s natural collagen production. It’s a great option for most patients in Houston who want gradual improvement that settles over time. This aesthetic treatment provides structural support and improved skin quality rather than an immediate and temporary artificial plump. What Aesthetic Issues Does Sculptra Address? Sculptra works to reinforce the skin’s structural foundation by addressing the biological signs of aging. At Heights Plastic Surgery and Studio Essex Medical Spa, our team uses this unique dermal filler to restore volume and skin thickness in specific facial areas: Facial Volume Loss: This Sculptra treatment rebuilds the collagen framework in the cheeks to correct hollowing and restore a youthful appearance. Deep Lines and Wrinkles: Sculptra is effective for smoothing deep nasolabial folds and marionette lines that run from the nose to the mouth. Jawline, Neck, and Temples: Sculptra recreates a natural appearance in the temples and along the jawline and neck, where lost collagen often leads to hollowing. Benefits of Sculptra Sculptra aesthetic treatment provides a biological increase in natural collagen that results in an authentic, refreshed physique. Patients choose this facial rejuvenation strategy for its durability and the natural-looking results that evolve over time: Long Lasting Facial Rejuvenation: Results can last up to two years, which is significantly longer than many other dermal fillers. Natural Progression: Changes occur gradually over a few months, ensuring the transformation is discreet. Stimulates Collagen Production: This injectable treatment addresses the root cause of sagging skin by helping the body produce collagen within its connective tissues. Improved Skin Texture: It improves skin texture and tone on most facial areas and can even be applied to specific body areas. Candidates for Sculptra Ideal Sculptra candidates are anyone concerned about volume loss or deep facial lines who want to achieve optimal results through a gradual process. Sculptra is suited for patients who have the patience for a result that builds over multiple sessions. Safety protocols at Heights Plastic Surgery and Studio Essex Medical Spa require us to screen your medical history for specific health factors. You should skip this treatment if you have a history of keloid formation or hypertrophic scarring. Our team reviews your medical history during the initial consultation to ensure your aesthetic goals can be met safely. Consultation and Preparation for Sculptra Your Houston Sculptra consultation includes a physical assessment of your bone structure and skin elasticity. Our clinical team of specialists determines if your anatomy will respond best to Sculptra, or if other non-surgical products and services, or even a plastic surgery procedure option would be a better option. Preparation is straightforward. Avoid taking blood thinners or supplements for one week to minimize the risk of mild swelling or bruising at the injection sites. We also recommend disclosing all medications to avoid potential risks during the healing process. The Sculptra Procedure The Sculptra treatment takes place at Heights Plastic Surgery and Studio Essex Medical Spa and usually lasts between 30 and 45 minutes. We apply a topical numbing cream or topical anesthetic to ensure you feel generally mild discomfort during the cosmetic procedures. Your provider uses a fine needle to place the formula into the underlying tissues in the treatment area. Related Treatments

Using a very fine needle, your highly skilled injector will carefully and competently inject Sculptra into the treatment area. Although you may notice some results immediately after treatment, results continue to maximize in the months to come. 4. Follow Up. After the Sculptra Houston facial filler specialists can schedule you for follow-up Sculptra injections or any other complementary procedures you'd like to opt for to enhance the effects of your dermal filler injections. Sculptra Recovery Sculptra requires no formal downtime, you can return to your daily life right away. You may notice mild swelling or redness at the injection sites, but this typically fades within a few weeks. We recommend that patients follow the 5-5-5 rule: massage the treatment area for five minutes, five times a day, for five days to ensure the treatment is evenly distributed. Sculptra Results Changes are not visible right away; optimal Sculptra results take time as the body works to produce collagen. Real results emerge as Sculptra stimulates your natural collagen production over a few months. Most patients see their desired results about three to six months after their final sessions. To achieve optimal results, we usually recommend a personalized treatment plan of three sessions. Sculptra Aftercare Rigorous sun protection is vital to extend your youthful skin, as UV rays break down the natural collagen you are building. Maintaining a healthy lifestyle supports the biological process required for collagen production. Regular touch-up visits every year or two help maintain long-lasting facial rejuvenation. How Much Does Sculptra Cost? The cost of Sculptra reflects the amount of product needed in your personalized treatment plan.
