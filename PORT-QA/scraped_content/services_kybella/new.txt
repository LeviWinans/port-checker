See Results What is a Kybella Procedure? Kybella is an FDA-approved, injectable treatment specifically engineered to target and eliminate a double chin. Ideal Candidates for Kybella Injections Schedule Today Chemical fat reduction requires strict anatomical criteria. A good candidate for Kybella is an adult who possesses a distinct pocket of submental fat and remarkably high skin elasticity. Targeted Fullness: You must be bothered by localized fat under the chin, not widespread neck fat or descended muscle bands. Strong Skin Retraction: As the fat dissolves, your skin must have the collagen reserves to shrink tightly against the new contour. If you possess severe loose skin, dissolving the fat will only exacerbate the sagging. In these instances, our surgeons will pivot the plan and recommend a formal neck lift instead. Medical Clearance: You must not have an active infection in the injection area, underlying bleeding problems, or difficulty swallowing. Realistic Expectations: Patients must understand that this revolutionary treatment is a multi-step process. It requires patience. How to Prepare for Your Kybella Consultation Every aesthetic intervention begins with a precise clinical assessment. During your Kybella consultation, we physically evaluate the tension of your skin and the density of your submental fullness. We determine if this chemical approach aligns with your specific anatomy. To prepare for the actual injections, you must halt all blood-thinning medications, NSAIDs (like ibuprofen), and certain herbal supplements one week prior to mitigate bleeding and heavy bruising. The Treatment Procedure We execute this non-surgical treatment with extreme precision. We first clean the neck and apply a potent topical numbing agent to the skin to ensure patient comfort. We then map out a highly specific grid of dots across the treatment area to guide the product distribution perfectly. Using a microscopic needle, we administer multiple small injections of the synthetic deoxycholic acid directly into the submental fat pad. The entire injection sequence takes less than twenty minutes. You will feel a deep, warm, aching sensation as the active ingredient immediately begins to dismantle the fat cell membranes. We apply ice immediately to soothe the skin.

Expected Kybella Results Kybella offers a permanent structural shift, but the biological clearing process takes time. You will not leave the office with a smaller chin. Over the following weeks, your immune system slowly flushes away the destroyed cells. Most patients require a series of sessions to achieve optimal, long-lasting results. Depending on the volume of the fat pad, we typically administer two to four treatments spaced about four to six weeks apart. We will never administer more than six treatments for safety and efficacy reasons. Once the final swelling subsides, the jawline emerges sharp, clean, and highly defined.

Recovery and Expected Downtime Schedule Today We do not sugarcoat the inflammatory response. This product works by creating controlled inflammation to destroy the cells. You must be prepared for the physical reality of the recovery phase. The Initial Swelling: Immediately after the injections, the area beneath your chin will swell significantly. This “bullfrog” effect is the exact chemical reaction proving the product is working. The swelling typically peaks within the first 48 hours. Many women and men wear a scarf or turtleneck during this phase to conceal the volume. Managing Discomfort: You may experience localized pain, redness, numbness, and a feeling of firmness or “jelly” in the treated area. This discomfort is temporary. The Resolution: The intense swelling begins to resolve slowly after the first week. By week three, the tissue softens. You can resume normal activities and exercise almost immediately after leaving the clinic, though you may prefer to avoid high-profile social events during the peak swelling phase. Aftercare for Optimal Contouring Proper aftercare following Kybella mitigates early discomfort and supports the clearance of the fat. We advise patients to sleep with their head elevated on an extra pillow for the first few nights to encourage fluid drainage away from the neck. You may apply ice packs to the neck intermittently for the first two days to reduce swelling and soothe the skin. Do not aggressively massage the injection area. Allow the deoxycholic acid to work precisely where we placed it.

Kybella Frequently Asked Questions What is Kybella? Kybella® is an injectable cosmetic treatment that helps patients get rid of their double chin once-and-for-all by melting away submental fat. Kybella gives patients a non-surgical and minimally invasive alternative to more invasive facial contouring procedures – such as neck liposuction – while yielding the same, potentially permanent results. Kybella’s active ingredient, deoxycholic acid, simply melts away targeted fat which is naturally eliminated from the body following treatment. This leaves Houston Kybella patients with a beautifully contoured and slimmed appearance without the downtime and recovery period that comes with traditional facial contouring methods. How much does Kybella cost in Houston? The average cost of your Kybella treatment in Houston typically starts at about $600. The total cost for your Kybella treatment will depend on the amount of product needed to help you achieve your desired results. At Heights Plastic Surgery, we accept cash and all major credit cards. We are also happy to offer financing through CareCredit® for patients. Because Kybella® is often more about cosmetic appearance and is less of medical necessity, it is typically not covered by most insurance plans. How does Kybella work? Kybella is an in-office procedure that typically takes about 45 minutes to complete. Before your Kybella treatment begins, chin fat reduction experts Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will apply a topical anesthetic to your treatment areas. This is to ensure you are as comfortable and pain-free as possible throughout the entirety of your treatment. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will then place a stencil under your chin to mark your targeted areas, then strategically make a series of Kybella injections under the chin. Because Kybella is a minimally invasive procedure, there is no formal downtime or recovery period associated with treatment. What results and downtime should I expect after Kybella? Because Kybella is a minimally invasive treatment, patients are able to return to their normal daily activities – including work – immediately following treatment. It is normal to experience some redness, swelling, tenderness, and bruising around your treated areas. These side effects will typically subside on their own within 24 to 48 hours of treatment. While you may see some results of their Kybella injections after just one treatment, many patients require between three to six treatments to see ultimate results. Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks will determine your personalized treatment plan during your one-on-one consultation. Am I a good candidate for Kybella?
