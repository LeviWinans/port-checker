Kybella What are Dermal Fillers? Dermal fillers are injectable gel substances, typically made of hyaluronic acid (HA), used to restore lost volume, smooth deep lines, and enhance facial contours. Unlike neurotoxins that relax muscles, fillers physically occupy space under the skin to lift and hydrate tissues from within. What Aesthetic Issues Do Dermal Fillers Address? A common misconception is that fillers simply “fill wrinkles.” In reality, we use them to correct structural deficiencies. Volume Loss: As we age, the fat pads in our cheeks slide downward. Filler acts as a scaffold to lift them back into position. Shadow Patterns: Deep folds around the mouth (nasolabial folds) create dark shadows. Placing filler deep in the cheek can lift the tissue, softening these shadows without adding bulk to the mouth. Facial Balancing: A weak chin or undefined jawline can be projected to balance the profile, often making the nose appear smaller or the neck tighter. Lip Asymmetry: We use precise microdroplets to correct uneven borders or volume deficits in the lips. Benefits of Dermal Fillers The primary advantage of modern dermal fillers is their versatility and reversibility (in the case of HA fillers). Immediate Gratification: You leave the chair with visible improvement. Collagen Stimulation: Certain biostimulatory fillers (like Sculptra or Radiesse) actually trigger your body to produce its own collagen over time. Hydration: Hyaluronic acid pulls water into the skin, improving texture and “glow” in the treated area. Customizable: We can layer different products—using a firm filler for the jawline and a soft, flexible filler for the lips—to ensure natural movement. Candidates for Dermal Fillers Most adults with visible volume loss or contour concerns are candidates. The “Tired” Look: If you sleep 8 hours but still look exhausted due to under-eye hollows. Preventative: Patients in their late 20s often use small amounts to maintain structure before deep lines form. Realistic Expectations: Filler corrects volume, but it does not remove excess skin. If you have significant sagging, a surgical facelift may be the better option. Health: You should be free of active infections (like cold sores) on the day of treatment. How to Prepare for Your Treatment Bruising is the biggest social risk. You can minimize this by preparing your blood vessels. 7 Days Prior: Stop taking blood thinners like Aspirin, Ibuprofen, Vitamin E, and Fish Oil (unless prescribed by a doctor). 48 Hours Prior: Avoid alcohol. It thins the blood and increases the likelihood of bruising. Day of Treatment: Arrive with a clean face. We will remove makeup, but arriving without it saves time. Eat a light meal beforehand to prevent lightheadedness.

What to Expect Before, During, and After Dermal Fillers in Houston 1. Attend a One-on-One Consultation. If you're interested in getting dermal fillers in Houston, you'll first meet with a Studio Essex provider. We analyze your facial animation—how you smile and speak—to ensure we choose a product that moves naturally with you. 2. Approve Your Treatment Plan. Houston dermal filler experts Drs. Downey and Bullocks collaborate on cases to advise on the ideal options. We build a personalized "facial mapping" plan that may include a combination of different fillers (Restylane, Juvederm, RHA) to achieve balance, not just volume. 3. The Injection Process. After applying a strong topical numbing cream for 20 minutes, your provider will cleanse the area. We use both needles and blunt-tipped cannulas. Cannulas push blood vessels aside rather than piercing them, significantly reducing bruising and improving safety. The actual injection time is often less than 30 minutes. 4. Follow Up. We advise waiting two full weeks for swelling to subside before judging the final result. At that point, we can schedule a follow-up to "perfect" the results if needed. Results: What to Expect Immediate: You will see the volume instantly, but you will also be swollen. Do not panic if you look “overfilled” on day one. The Settling Period: Hyaluronic acid takes about 2 weeks to integrate with your tissue and pull in water. The result will soften and look more natural during this time. Longevity: Lips: 6–12 months (high movement area). Cheeks/Jawline: 12–24 months (deeper placement). Under-eyes: 12+ months. Recovery & Aftercare Recovery from dermal fillers is primarily social. You can drive yourself home and return to work, though you may have minor swelling. No Touching: Do not massage the treated area unless instructed. You want the product to stay exactly where we placed it. Stay Upright: Avoid lying flat for 4 hours post-injection to prevent swelling. No Heat: Avoid saunas, hot tubs, or intense cardio for 24 hours, as heat exacerbates swelling. Cold Compress: Intermittent icing helps reduce inflammation on the day of treatment. Cost of Dermal Fillers At Heights Plastic Surgery (Studio Essex), filler is priced by the syringe. The cost of dermal fillers reflects the quality of the product and the expertise of the injector. Range: Typically $675 – $900 per syringe, depending on the specific formulation (e.g., Voluma vs. Versa). Variables: A lip treatment may require 1 syringe, while full facial balancing (cheeks, chin, jawline) may require 3–5 syringes. Financing: We offer financing options through CareCredit and PatientFi to make comprehensive facial balancing accessible.

Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About Dermal Fillers in Houston What is the difference between Botox & Dermal Fillers? Botox (neurotoxin) relaxes muscles to stop wrinkles from forming. Dermal fillers restore volume to fill wrinkles that are already there. Think of Botox as a preventer and Filler as a restorer. They work best together. Will I look fake or “overdone”? Only if too much product is used. Our philosophy is “under-correction.” We prefer to add less initially and layer more later. The “duck lip” or “pillow face” look comes from poor placement and overfilling, which we strictly avoid. Does Dermal Fillers hurt? Most patients rate the pain a 2/10. We use a prescription-grade numbing cream, and the fillers themselves contain Lidocaine, which numbs the area internally as we inject. What brands of filler do you offer? We carry a curated selection of the top FDA-approved fillers: Restylane® Family: Excellent for precise lip definition and under-eye correction. Juvéderm® Family: Known for robust lifting capacity in the cheeks and jawline. RHA® Collection: Designed for dynamic areas like the mouth; it stretches and recoils with your facial expressions. Radiesse®: A calcium-based filler that provides structural definition to the jawline.
