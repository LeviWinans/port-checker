“Working in the cosmetic industry, I wanted to ensure that the surgeon I decided upon was not only the best, but came highly recommended by my boss. Dr. Villano was that surgeon.” Dr.Villano Dr. Michael Villano of VillanoMD is a facial plastic and reconstructive surgeon practicing in Bend, Oregon. Dr. Villano received his medical training at Wake Forest University School of Medicine. After obtaining his M.D. in 1995, he went on to complete an Otolaryngology and Head and Neck Surgery residency followed by a fellowship in Facial Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery at the University of Rochester School of Medicine. Prior to his medical training, Dr. Villano received his Bachelor of Science in Bioengineering at the University of California at San Diego while on a Navy Scholarship. Upon graduation, he entered the prestigious Navy Nuclear Propulsion Program, where he served as a nuclear submarine officer aboard the USS Haddo and the USS Hawkbill and earned the Navy Achievement Medal with Gold Star, the Navy Expeditionary Medal, and the Defense Service Medal honors. Dr. Villano has been practicing in Central Oregon since 2001.
