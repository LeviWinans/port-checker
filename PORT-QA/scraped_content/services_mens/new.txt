Men’s Services Recovery and Results Recovery times vary based on the type of treatment: Non-surgical treatments: Minimal to no downtime. Results from neurotoxins or fillers can be seen within a few days, with full effects in 1-2 weeks. Skincare and Hydrafacials show immediate improvements in skin texture and hydration. Surgical procedures: Depending on the procedure, recovery may take several days to a few weeks. Liposuction patients can expect to resume light activities within a few days, with full recovery in 4-6 weeks. Swelling and bruising are common but gradually subside over time. Scar care and follow-up appointments will be part of the recovery plan. To maintain results, follow post-treatment guidelines such as avoiding sun exposure, using recommended skincare products, and attending regular maintenance sessions for treatments like hair restoration or body sculpting. Periodic touch-ups may be needed to prolong results from injectables or other non-surgical procedures.
