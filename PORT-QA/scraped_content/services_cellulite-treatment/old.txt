View All Before & Afters What Is Cellulite Treatment? At our practice in Houston cellulite treatment with EmTone® is a noninvasive procedure that combines radiofrequency and targeted pressure energy to improve dimpled skin, mild laxity, and uneven texture. The two forms of energy are delivered simultaneously to address several structural factors that contribute to cellulite. Cellulite develops when fat beneath the skin presses upward while fibrous connective bands pull downward. Skin quality, circulation, genetics, hormones, age, and tissue structure can all influence its appearance. This is why cellulite can remain visible regardless of weight, fitness level, or commitment in the gym. EmTone takes a layered approach. Radiofrequency provides controlled heat to support collagen and elastin remodeling, while targeted pressure energy delivers mechanical stimulation to the underlying tissue. The treatment is designed to smooth the skin’s appearance without incisions, injections, or social downtime. What Aesthetic Issues Does Cellulite Treatment Address? EmTone is primarily used for cellulite and mild skin laxity. It can be applied to areas where dimpling, rippling, or uneven texture makes the skin look less smooth. With cellulite treatment Houston patients may improvement in: Cellulite on the thighs and buttocks Dimpled or orange-peel skin texture Mild skin laxity Uneven texture after weight changes Skin that appears less firm with age Soft cellulite visible when standing Firmer dimples caused by fibrous connective bands Areas where cellulite remains despite diet and exercise Cellulite treatment is not a weight-loss procedure, and EmTone is not designed to remove substantial fat. Patients seeking fat reduction, muscle definition, or surgical body contouring may need a different treatment or a combined plan. Benefits of EmTone Cellulite Treatment EmTone treats the surface appearance of cellulite and some of the structural factors beneath it during the same appointment. Its noninvasive format also makes it easy to fit into a full Houston schedule. Am I a Candidate for Cellulite Treatment? Good Candidates EmTone cellulite treatment may be appropriate for healthy adults who want to soften cellulite or improve mild laxity without surgery or injections. You may be a good candidate if you: Have visible cellulite on the thighs or buttocks Are bothered by uneven or dimpled skin Want a nonsurgical treatment with no social downtime Maintain realistic expectations about improvement Can complete the recommended treatment series Understand that cellulite cannot be permanently eliminated Are willing to return for maintenance when needed Body weight alone does not determine candidacy. Cellulite affects a wide range of body types, including patients who are lean, athletic, or at a stable weight. Who May Not Be a Good Fit Treatment may need to be postponed or avoided during pregnancy or when there is an active infection, open wound, or significant irritation in the treatment area. Certain implanted medical devices, metal implants, medical conditions, medications, or sensory changes may also affect candidacy. Tell your provider about your complete medical history, previous procedures, current medications, and any implanted devices. Studio Essex will determine whether EmTone can be performed safely or whether another option would be more appropriate. Patients with substantial loose skin, larger localized fat deposits, or more advanced body-contouring concerns may not achieve their desired result with EmTone alone. How Should I Prepare for Cellulite Treatment? EmTone cellulite treatment requires little preparation, but a few practical steps can make the appointment more comfortable. Share your medical history. Tell your provider about health conditions, implanted devices, medications, and previous treatments in the area. Keep the skin calm. Avoid arriving with a sunburn, rash, open wound, or freshly irritated skin. Stay hydrated. Good hydration supports circulation and helps you feel comfortable before and after treatment. Wear comfortable clothing. Choose clothing that can be moved easily away from the thighs, buttocks, abdomen, or other treatment areas. Remove topical products. Arrive with clean skin free of self-tanner, heavy lotion, body oil, or fragranced products, unless directed otherwise. Plan around the full series. Consistent scheduling helps keep the treatment plan moving and supports a more predictable progression. No fasting, anesthesia preparation, or transportation arrangement is generally required. What Is the EmTone Cellulite Treatment Procedure Like? Your appointment begins with an assessment of the treatment area. Your provider will evaluate the location, pattern, and severity of cellulite before confirming the settings and session plan. A conductive lotion is applied to help the handpiece move smoothly across the skin. The device then delivers radiofrequency and targeted pressure energy at the same time. Radiofrequency warms the tissue, while the mechanical energy creates rapid pulses or vibrations beneath the handpiece. Most patients compare the experience to a warm, energetic massage. The heat builds gradually, and the targeted pressure creates a rhythmic tapping sensation. Numbing cream is not typically necessary. Your provider monitors your comfort and the tissue response throughout the appointment. A session usually takes approximately 20 to 30 minutes, depending on the size and number of areas treated. Once the procedure is complete, the lotion is removed, and you can return to the rest of your day.
