See Results What is Hair Restoration? Hair restoration is a treatment approach designed to address visible thinning, shedding, and reduced density in areas where the hair no longer looks as full as it once did. Depending on the patient, that may mean improving the appearance of the hairline, supporting density through the scalp, or creating a more strategic plan for long-term maintenance. At Heights Plastic Surgery in Houston hair restoration is best approached as a tailored process rather than a one-size-fits-all service. Hair loss can be subtle at first. A widening part, more scalp visibility near the temples, or thinning through the crown often changes the way the face is framed before patients realize how much density has shifted. The goal is to support healthier growth, improve the appearance of fullness, and create a plan that fits the pattern of loss and the condition of the scalp.

Candidates Schedule Today Hair restoration can be a strong option for patients who are noticing thinning, shedding, or a gradual loss of density and want a more thoughtful treatment plan. The best candidates are often the patients who address hair changes early. Waiting until loss becomes more advanced can limit which options are most effective. You may be a good candidate if you: are seeing early or moderate hair thinning want to support density before loss becomes more advanced are bothered by scalp visibility have concerns about your hairline, temples, or crown are in good general health are willing to follow a treatment plan consistently have realistic expectations about gradual improvement How to Prepare for Hair Restoration Preparation begins with a thorough consultation and a close look at the scalp, pattern of thinning, and overall hair health. Before hair restoration treatments Houston patients should be prepared to discuss when the hair changes began, how quickly they progressed, and whether there is a family history of hair loss. Procedure Hair restoration procedures vary based on the treatment selected and the patient’s needs. Some approaches are focused on stimulating the scalp and supporting healthier follicles. Others are designed to improve the appearance of density through targeted treatment in areas where thinning is more visible. The appointment itself is typically straightforward. After the scalp is evaluated and the treatment plan is confirmed, the selected method is performed in a controlled clinical setting. Depending on the approach, treatment may focus on the hairline, crown, temples, or diffuse thinning throughout the scalp. What matters most is not just the procedure itself, but choosing the right protocol and setting realistic expectations for progression. Hair restoration works best when it is treated as a process rather than a quick fix.

Results Hair restoration results usually take time. Hair growth moves slowly, and visible improvement tends to happen gradually rather than all at once. Some patients begin to see early changes within the first few months, while fuller improvement may take longer. The exact timeline depends on the treatment plan, the starting point, and how the scalp responds. Patients may notice: Less visible thinning over time Improved appearance of density Better scalp coverage A fuller-looking hairline or crown Hair that appears healthier and stronger Improved styling flexibility as density improves

Hair Restoration Frequently Asked Questions What is hair restoration? Hair restoration is a treatment that helps strengthen your natural hair to fill in areas of thinning. Houston hair restoration experts Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullock offer advanced, cutting-edge treatments for men and women. The results look and feel completely natural. At Studio Essex, we accept cash and all major credit cards. We are also proud to offer financing options for our patients through CareCredit®. How do you perform hair restoration? Not everyone needs extensive hair transplant surgery to correct thinning hair. Genetics play an important role in how men and women lose their hair. Specifically, the growth cycle of your hair shortens, and this leaves areas with little to no hair, as well as hair strands that are weak. Houston hair restoration experts Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks use proven, advanced, cutting-edge approaches to stimulate a normal, healthy hair growth cycle. The new hair strands gradually grow out thicker and stronger in a natural growth pattern. What can I expect during my hair restoration recovery? The hair restoration techniques used by Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks require no downtime. There are no incisions, and you won’t develop any scarring. You may need follow-up treatments to maintain the results. Men and women who undergo hair restoration should avoid over-processing the hair to protect against additional hair loss. Am I a good candidate for hair restoration? The first step in determining candidacy for hair restoration in Houston is to rule out any other factors that may be affecting your hair growth, such as hormonal imbalances, stress, over-processing, medications, and other lifestyle factors. The ideal candidates for our hair restoration treatments are men who have male-pattern baldness and women who have female-pattern baldness. Male-pattern baldness typically starts with thinning in the temple area, and the hair loss expands to the crown of the head. Female-pattern baldness is a generalized thinning that occurs over the entire scalp. Why should I choose hair restoration in Houston at Studio Essex?
