Body Contouring Frequently Asked Questions What is EmSculpt NEO Body Contouring? Emsculpt NEO is a revolutionary body contouring device that provides muscle stimulation in a strategic manner, helping you to achieve your goals faster. The NEO is the most cutting-edge version of its predecessor, Emsculpt. This treatment is unique in that it emits both radiofrequency and high intensity electromagnetic energy, resulting in simultaneous fat burning and muscle building! How does EmSculpt NEO work? The Emsculpt NEO body contouring applicator emits a powerful combination of radiofrequency and high intensity electromagnetic energy (HIFEM), which provides two effective methods of muscle toning. The radiofrequency quickly heats the the muscles, raising the temperature by several degrees. This heat reaches the subcutaneous fat cells, which permanently damages them. Your body then removes them as waste. Additionally, the HIFEM is working to contract your muscles at a rate not possible from exercise alone. These intense contractions result in the fast paced growth of muscle fibers in the treatment area! Who is an ideal candidate for EmSculpt NEO? EmSculpt NEO Body Contouring should be used an an additional tool for a healthy lifestyle, and is not intended as a weight loss program. The treatment is ideal for those within 10% to 15% of their ideal body weight who workout consistently and are otherwise healthy! Emsculpt NEO is a fantastic way to break down those stubborn areas of fat in the abdomen and buttocks that otherwise seem resistant to diet and exercise alone. How long does it take to see Body Contouring results? Much like a traditional workout, results are not immediate, but they are noticeable. You can expect to see results from your first Emsculpt NEO body contouring treatment in 2 to 4 weeks!
