Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About WiQo Peel Facial Rejuvenation Treatment What is a WiQo Peel? WiQo® Peel is a non-peeling advanced aesthetic treatment that dramatically revitalizes skin without harsh solutions or needles. WiQo® combines a special patented serum and a specialized application technique that goes deep into the sub-dermal layers of the skin to help stimulate collagen production and promote skin renewal. WiQo® Peel is often referred to as a “facelift in a bottle.” However, WiQo® Peels can be performed on the face as well as different areas of the body that show signs of aging or trauma in the form of scarring. How much does a WiQo Peel cost in Houston? The cost of your WiQo® Peel treatment will be dependent on a number of factors, including what areas are being treated, how many areas are treated, and how many treatment sessions are necessary. At Heights Plastic Surgery, we accept cash and all major credit cards. We are also happy to offer financing through CareCredit® for our patients. How does a WiQo Peel work? Your WiQo® Peel treatment will begin with a deep, delicate cleansing of the treatment area. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will then apply the WiQo® Peel serum solution, massaging it into the skin. Once the determined number of layers has been applied, the serum residue is washed from your skin. A post-application cream may be used to help ease any slight burning or itching sensations. What should I expect after my WiQo Peel? Unlike other chemical peels, there is no downtime with a WiQo® Peel treatment. Immediately following your WiQo® Peel, you can resume your normal daily activities. You can expect to see bright, tighter, and plumper-looking skin. If applied to your face, you should see a more rested and glowing complexion. There may be slight flaking and redness as your skin continues to shed excess skin, but there will be no peeling as what typically follows a traditional peel. Am I a good candidate for a WiQo Peel? Ideal candidates for WiQo® Peel treatments are in good general health and have realistic expectations of their potential results. WiQo® Peels are great alternatives for individuals who desire to achieve a smoother, more youthful appearance without having to have surgery or undergo needles and more invasive chemical peel treatments. WiQo® Peel can treat the following skin conditions: Uneven skin tone and texture Skin laxity Fine lines and wrinkles Scarring Age spots and sun damage
