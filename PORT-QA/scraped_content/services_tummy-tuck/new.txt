What is a Tummy Tuck? Abdominoplasty, popularly known as the Tummy Tuck, is a surgical cosmetic procedure that addresses the loose, sagging skin around a man or woman’s midsection. In Houston, Texas, Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks perform a Tummy Tuck to safely remove the excess skin and fat from the abdomen and address aesthetic concerns due to aging, heredity, pregnancy, or significant fluctuations in weight. This procedure can restore your appearance and repair weakened and separated muscles in the midsection. We commonly combine abdominoplasty with liposuction and skin tightening techniques to help provide a harmonious shape of the trunk and a smaller waistline. This is important to planning our Tummy Tucks because our goal is to treat the entire front and back to create a smaller waistline. Board-certified plastic surgeons Dr. Cara Downey and Dr. Jamal Bullocks have welcomed hundreds of patients who wish to achieve smoother and firmer abdominal profiles through their expertise.

What Tummy Tuck techniques do you offer? Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks may use any of the following techniques to contour your body with a Tummy Tuck. Traditional Tummy Tuck If you want to improve your upper and lower abdomen, the traditional or full Tummy Tuck repairs the problem area with a horizontal or U-shaped incision made from hip bone-to-hip bone and just over the pubic hairline. Then, excess skin is removed while abdominal muscles are tightened. Though it yields a longer scar than other surgical methods, a full Tummy Tuck gives you a flatter, smoother stomach. This results in increased comfort in pants and skirts, plus more flattering silhouettes in form-fitting clothes. Mini Tummy Tuck If your issue is limited to just a protruding or unflattering lower belly, a mini Tummy Tuck may be enough to address it. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will make one short incision beneath the panty line. Loose muscles in the lower abdomen are tightened, and excess skin is removed to flatten your abdominal wall. The mini Tummy Tuck is relatively quicker than the traditional procedure, requires a shorter recovery, and produces a flattering lower belly. Liposuction of the abdomen is commonly performed to sculpt and improve the overall results. Extended Tummy Tuck For patients who desire the removal of loose abdominal skin from a wider area, the extended procedure could be a better fit than the traditional technique. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks makes a longer incision that extends from the hip to the sides of the lower back and removes the patient’s excess skin. This technique is often recommended for patients who have undergone massive weight loss. Circumferential Tummy Tuck This technique gets its name from the fact that the incision is made all around the waistline, spanning the entire lower body. The technique, also known as a lower body lift, is recommended by Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks to patients who have undergone extreme weight loss or have sagging of the outer thighs and buttocks that need to be addressed. The aim of the circumferential approach is to remove an abundance of excess fat and loose skin in problem areas such as the tummy, outer thighs, and buttocks. Tummy Tuck Benefits Undergoing a Tummy Tuck can significantly enhance your quality of life by addressing various concerns and achieving aesthetic goals. Tummy TuckFrequently Asked Questions How much does a Tummy Tuck cost in Houston? The cost of a Tummy Tuck in Houston usually falls in the range of $8,000 to $12,000 on average. The final amount you pay for the procedure will be based on factors such as the fee charged by your surgeon, where the procedure is performed, and any additional medical expenses that may arise. We accept all major credit cards and cash payments. Additionally, we offer financing options through CareCredit®. Keep in mind that Tummy Tuck surgery is considered a cosmetic procedure and is not typically covered by insurance plans. What can I expect during my recovery after a Tummy Tuck? For recovery after a Tummy Tuck in Houston patients will typically have some initial pain, bruising, soreness, and swelling, which can be managed by medication and the use of a compression garment. Dr. Downey and Dr. Bullocks will provide personalized recovery guidance. In two to three weeks, most patients can return to light daily activities, with strenuous exercise restricted for six to eight weeks. While significant improvement is visible in one to three months, ongoing swelling may subside over the next several months. Final results are best maintained with a healthy diet and exercise. Our surgeons also have expertise in performing revision surgery for anyone who has had a previous Tummy Tuck and is in need of additional body contouring. Can I combine a Tummy Tuck with any other procedures? Yes, a Tummy Tuck can be combined with other cosmetic procedures to achieve comprehensive body contouring and rejuvenation. Common combination procedures include liposuction to further sculpt the body and breast augmentation or lift for enhanced overall symmetry and proportion. What are the risks of a Tummy Tuck? While Tummy Tucks are generally safe when performed by a qualified plastic surgeon, as with any surgical procedure, there are inherent risks. These may include infection, bleeding, adverse reactions to anesthesia, scarring, asymmetry, and changes in sensation. However, by carefully following pre-operative and post-operative instructions and choosing a skilled surgeon, these risks can be minimized. Is a Tummy Tuck permanent? While a Tummy Tuck can provide long-lasting results, it’s essential to understand that the aging process and lifestyle factors can affect the outcome over time. Maintaining a regular exercise routine, a healthy diet, and stable weight can help preserve the results for years to come. Can I get pregnant following a Tummy Tuck? While it is possible to become pregnant after a Tummy Tuck, it’s generally recommended to postpone the procedure until you have completed your family. Pregnancy following a Tummy Tuck can potentially undo the results of the surgery, necessitating further procedures to restore your desired aesthetic outcome. It’s best to discuss your future plans for pregnancy with your plastic surgeon during your consultation to ensure the timing is appropriate for your goals.
