Breast lift surgery, also known as mastopexy, removes excess and sagging tissue from the breasts. This tissue usually develops as a result of massive weight loss, breastfeeding, or even the effects of gravity over time. As a mother of five, Dr. Cara Downey understands these aesthetic concerns’ impact on the body. Dr. Downey’s and Dr. Bullocks’ attention to detail and exceptional patient care has allowed them to perform more than 100 successful breast lift procedures at Heights Plastic Surgery. Am I a Candidate? Good candidates for a breast lift are patients who are in good general health. Women above the age of 40 or those with a significant family history of breast cancer must have a mammogram before surgery. Additionally, women who experience irregularities such as nipple discharge, skin dimpling, or lumps will also need to undergo screening tests to ensure their safety. Ideal candidates for a breast lift experience any of the following concerns: Sagging breast skin Deflated breasts due to pregnancy, breastfeeding, and massive weight loss Low-lying nipples Breast asymmetry Aesthetic Issues Addressed by Breast Lift A breast lift can address several common issues that may be affecting your self-esteem and comfort. Here’s how it helps: Sagging or Drooping Breasts: Lifts and firms your breasts, giving them a perkier look. Loss of Breast Firmness and Elasticity: Tightens the breast tissue for a firmer feel. Low Nipple Position or Downward-Pointing Nipples: Repositions your nipples to a more youthful height. Asymmetry Between Breasts: Balances the size and shape of your breasts. Stretched or Enlarged Areolas: Reduces the size of the areolas to better fit your new breast shape. Benefits of a Breast Lift A breast lift does more than improve your appearance—it can enhance your overall quality of life. This important step ensures you are healing properly, safely, and efficiently. How does a breast lift work? A breast lift is an outpatient procedure that typically takes about two to four hours to complete. To begin, your provider will administer general anesthesia to ensure you are comfortable and pain-free throughout your surgery. Houston breast lift surgeons, Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks, will then raise your breasts and reposition their tissues, skin, nipples, and areolas to a higher position on the chest wall. The nipples will be kept intact and remain connected to the breast. Breast implants may be inserted after Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks has sculpted your breasts to their new shape and contour. After the incisions are closed, they will protect the treatment areas with a supportive bra and surgical dressings. Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks may use any of the following techniques to lift and re-contour your breasts during your mastopexy: Classic Anchor Lift (WISE Pattern): We recommend this technique for women with a large amount of excess skin. We will make an incision around the nipple, and a vertical line will then extend downwards to the breast fold across the underside of the breast. Lollipop Lift: This technique is ideal for women with minimal excess skin. A smaller incision will be made around the nipple, extending downwards towards the base of the breast. Peri-Areolar Lift: If the breast mound is already in a good position, we will reposition the nipple with minimal tightening. In most cases, the incision is only limited to the areola’s circumference. Results of a Breast Lift The results of a breast lift are often immediately noticeable. Your breasts will be lifted and more youthful in appearance right after the procedure. As the swelling subsides and your body heals, the full benefits will become more evident, giving you a refreshed and confident look. Recovery from a Breast Lift For the first 24 hours following your breast lift Houston patients typically experience moderate chest pain and nausea due to the anesthesia used during surgery. You may also remove your surgical dressings and tubes 48 hours after surgery. After two to three days following your breast lift, you may resume showering as normal. We recommend avoiding the use of narcotic pain medication after three days. Within two weeks, you may return to work and resume low-impact daily activities and exercises, provided your breasts are supported. At this time, most of your pain and soreness will begin to subside. About 6 to 8 weeks following surgery, Dr. Downey or Dr. Bullocks will suggest you remove your compression garments and start to wear normal bras again. We recommend seeking out bras that do not have an underwire, as an underwire may irritate your scars. At this time, you may resume normal activities and workout routines without hesitation. Enter Email* Δ Frequently Asked Questions About Breast Lifts in Houston What is the difference between a breast lift and breast augmentation? A breast lift reshapes and lifts sagging breasts without significantly changing their size, while breast augmentation involves using implants to increase breast size. Some patients may choose to combine both procedures for optimal results. How long does a breast lift procedure take? The procedure typically takes between two to four hours, depending on the complexity of the surgery and any additional treatments being performed. Will there be visible scars after a breast lift? While some scarring is inevitable, our skilled surgeons use advanced techniques to minimize scarring and place incisions in discreet locations. Over time, scars tend to fade and become less noticeable. How long do the results of a breast lift last? Results can last many years, but factors such as aging, gravity, and significant weight changes can affect longevity. Maintaining a stable weight and healthy lifestyle can help prolong your results. Can I breastfeed after a breast lift? Many women can still breastfeed after a breast lift, but it’s not guaranteed. It depends on the type of lift and the extent of tissue manipulation. Discuss your breastfeeding plans with your surgeon during the consultation. What type of anesthesia is used during a breast lift? A breast lift is typically performed under general anesthesia, ensuring you are comfortable and pain-free during the procedure. Are there non-surgical alternatives to a breast lift? While non-surgical treatments like skin tightening and fillers can provide subtle improvements, they cannot achieve the same results as a surgical breast lift. Surgery is the most effective option for significant lifting and reshaping. How should I prepare for my breast lift surgery? Preparation includes a medical evaluation, possibly adjusting medications, quitting smoking, and following specific pre-operative instructions provided by your surgeon. Detailed preparation helps ensure a smooth surgery and recovery process. What are the risks associated with a breast lift? As with any surgery, a breast lift carries some risks, including infection, bleeding, and changes in nipple or breast sensation. Choosing a board-certified and experienced surgeon like those at Heights Plastic Surgery can minimize these risks. How soon can I return to work after a breast lift? Most patients can return to work and normal activities within one to two weeks, depending on the nature of their job and how quickly they heal. Your surgeon will provide personalized advice based on your recovery progress.
