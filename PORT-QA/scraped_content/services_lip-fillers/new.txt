What Are Lip Fillers? Lip fillers, also known as lip augmentation or lip plumping, involve injecting hyaluronic acid-based substances into the lips. These fillers enhance lip volume, restore lost fullness due to aging, and improve facial symmetry. Common brands include Restylane Kiss, Restylane, Juvederm, and RHA. Lip fillers are designed to give you plumper, fuller lips, correct asymmetries, and smooth out fine lines around your mouth. The procedure is quick, usually taking about 45 minutes to an hour, and is perfect for anyone 18 and older who isn’t pregnant or breastfeeding.

Results and Recovery Schedule Today You’ll see the results from lip fillers right away, with full effects showing after two to four days as the filler settles. Expect some initial swelling and bruising, but these typically go away within a week. You can get back to your normal activities immediately, but avoid strenuous exercise and too much sun for the first 24 hours. Following aftercare instructions is key to getting the best results.
