BodyTite BodyTite is designed for larger treatment areas of the body, such as the abdomen, upper arms, thighs, buttocks, and knees. Patients choose this option when they want to tighten skin and reduce stubborn fat in regions that don’t respond to diet and exercise. BodyTite can also be combined with liposuction to achieve smoother contours and help the skin contract more effectively, making it a strong alternative for those not ready for surgery. FaceTite FaceTite offers patients an option for facial rejuvenation that does not involve a full facelift. Using the same radiofrequency technology, it tightens sagging skin along the lower face and neck, sharpens the jawline, and reduces wrinkles or jowls. With only a tiny incision beneath the chin or around the ears, FaceTite can rejuvenate these areas with minimal downtime and without the risks of general anesthesia. AccuTite AccuTite is the most precise of the three modalities and is ideal for smaller or delicate areas of the face and body. Patients often use AccuTite to address concerns under the eyes, along the brows, around the nasolabial folds, or at the jawline. Because it works on such fine areas, AccuTite allows our surgeons to refine details that contribute to a more youthful, natural appearance.
