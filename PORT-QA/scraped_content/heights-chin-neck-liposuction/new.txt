What Is A Neck Liposuction And Chin Liposuction? Sometimes stubborn pockets of fat around our bodies can be resistant to diet and exercise—no matter how strict we might be. A particularly stubborn area can be the chin and neck area. At Heights Plastic Surgery, Dr. Downey uses liposuction to manually remove by suction fat cells and deposits of tissue to sculpt and contour the neck, chin, and jawline area for a more contoured look. It’s a safe, effective, quick treatment to help patients permanently remove extra fat that’s been holding them back. Each and every patient receives a customized treatment plan to fit a patient’s individual needs. FROM CONSULT TO SURGERY DAY FOR YOUR NECK AND CHIN Liposuction At your one-on-one consultation, you’ll meet with Dr. Downey and her patient care coordinator to discuss all your options and create a customized treatment plan. She will review your health history, do a physical exam, go thru each procedure option step-by-step to create a customized plan and quote just for you. You’ll have ample time to have any questions answered, review any of your concerns and view before and after photos. At Heights Plastic Surgery your neck and chin liposuction procedure is typically performed with twilight anesthesia ensuring you are comfortable and pain-free throughout your surgery, you will be able to return home the same day as surgery. The surgery will take place in Dr. Downey’s private AAAASF facility which is considered the gold standard in accreditation of free-standing ambulatory surgery centers. On the day of surgery, she will mark on your face where the small incisions will go and thoroughly review her surgical plan with you before anesthesia is administered. For you the surgery will happen in a blink of an eye, but most facial liposuction surgeries take between 1 – 3 hours. When your surgery is complete you will be monitored in a recovery room by our caring and knowledgeable staff until you are released to a loved one for transportation home to continue to recover. We will see you back in-office usually 24 to 72 hours after surgery to ensure you are healing well and see you for weekly visits until 6 weeks post-op, when you are typically released to return to all activities.
