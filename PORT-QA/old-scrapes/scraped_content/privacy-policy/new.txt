Introduction This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes how affiliated entities HRC Fertility Management, LLC, Huntington Reproductive Center Medical Group, a Medical Corporation dba HRC Fertility, NexGenomics, LLC, Reproductive Surgical Associates dba RSA, and related affiliated entities (collectively, “us,” “we,” “our,” or “HRC”), collect, use, share, and protect information that is obtained from and about visitors to our Websites and App. We respect Your privacy rights and hope that this Policy gives You confidence as You visit and use the Site, and demonstrates our commitment to fair information practices and the protection of privacy. Scope of this Policy This Policy covers our online data collection activities, including information that we collect through our Websites and App. If You are one of our valued Patients, then You can learn more about our privacy practices for our Patients with the Notice of Privacy Practices that You received when You joined as a Patient or it can be found on our Websites and App, available at https://havingbabies.com/hipaa-privacy-notice/. By continuing to visit and use our Websites and App, You are agreeing to the terms of this Policy. If You do not agree, then please exit this Website and close Your browser, or close the App and delete the same from Your device. Definitions “Patient(s)” means a natural person who is receiving services from HRC. “Prospective Patient(s)” means a natural person who requests a consultation with or has submitted a new patient appointment/consultation request with HRC. “Personal Data” means Personal Information. “Personal Information” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual that is collected from our Patients and Users via our Website and App. Personal Information may include a Patient’s or a User’s: full name, physical address, email address, IP address, or other identifier such as from a Cookie. Personal Information does not include any aggregate or anonymized data that may have been created from Personal Information but can no longer be used to identify, relate to, or could reasonably be linked to a Patient or a User. “Sensitive Personal Information” means any information collected from our Patients and Users that may reveal a racial or ethnic origin, political opinion, religious belief, philosophical belief, or trade union membership. It also may include a natural person’s genetic and biometric data, financial information, and data concerning their health, sex life, or sexual orientation. “User(s)” means a natural person who visits our Websites and App. “You” or “Your” means a Prospective Patient, a Patient, and a User. “Websites and App” means https://havingbabies.com/ and any of our other websites that may be developed over time, including, but not limited to, our patient portals, billing/financial portals, and electronic medical records portals and related mobile applications. Our fertility specialists are recognized throughout the world as leaders in patient care, innovation, and research. We strive to provide world class service and surgical facilities to our Patients, and management, laboratory, and surgical facilities support to their healthcare providers, while respecting their privacy throughout the journey. For Users who are just visiting our Websites and App, as well as Prospective Patients, we also respect and value Your privacy as well. It is our policy to only obtain the Personal Information that is absolutely necessary to provide our products and services to You and to limit access to Your Personal Information to only those individuals who need it to serve Your needs, provide You with the most comprehensive and personalized online experience possible, and in connection with Your preferences. Additionally, as we strive to provide the best service and experience, certain information may also be used to maintain and improve our customer service to You, or be used to create and develop new products, offerings, and events, and otherwise support the provision of our products and services to our Patients. Cookies: When You visit our Websites and App, we may assign Your device one or more cookies to facilitate access to our site and to personalize Your online experience. Through the use of a cookie, we also may automatically collect information about Your online activity on our site, such as the web pages You visit, the links You click, and the searches You conduct on our site. Most browsers automatically accept cookies, but You can usually modify Your browser setting to decline cookies. A cookie is a small text file that is stored on a user’s computer for record keeping purposes. Cookies can be either session cookies or persistent cookies. A session cookie expires when You close Your browser and is used to make it easier for You to navigate our Websites and App. A persistent cookie remains on Your hard drive for an extended period of time. When You first visit our site, we provide You with a choice to either accept or deny certain cookies. If You choose to decline cookies please note that some of the features and services of our Websites and/or App may not function as well as intended. Even if You choose to accept cookies, You are always free to change Your mind and to revoke that consent. For more information or to opt out of the use of cookies even if you have already consented to the same, please click on the link at the bottom right of Your screen that states “Cookie Policy” or by clicking on our cookie banner located on our Website and/or App, or by visiting https://havingbabies.com/cookie_policy/. We may use cookies to personalize content and ads, to provide social media features and to analyze our traffic. We may also share information about Your use of our site with our social media, advertising and analytics partners, who may combine it with other information that You’ve provided to them or that they’ve collected from Your use of their services. These analytics providers may use technologies such as cookies, web server logs, and web beacons to help us analyze Users’ use of our Websites and App. The information collected through these technologies, including, but not limited to, Your IP address, may be disclosed to the analytics providers and other relevant third parties that use information to analyze the use of the Websites and App. Google Analytics: We may use Google Analytics to collect information to improve our Websites and App. In order to collect this information, Google Analytics may set cookies on Your device, or read cookies that are already there. We may utilize Google Tag Manager to manage and organize all tags on our Websites and App, and to control when those tags are triggered. You can view more information about Google Tag Manager’s privacy practices, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en. Meta Pixel: We may use Meta Pixel to customize our advertising and to serve You ads on Your social media based on Your browsing behavior.
